- The Activity Monitor's "Approve & merge as maintainer" on a PR that needs a rebase (behind or conflicting with its base) becomes **Rebase, then merge as maintainer** instead of failing with a GitHub 405: the click records your approval first, has the hive rebase the PR, waits for CI, then merges it with your approval on record — the row reads *Rebasing → merge queued (approved by @you)*, the ledger's `followed[]` carries each step, and a conflict settles as *failed* with the conflicting files and the options *Adopt into fresh branch* / *Close as superseded*. The approval stays bound to the head you read: only a head the hive itself committed counts as the rebase, and a head anyone else pushes after your click settles the follow-through as *failed* (`foreign-head`) with nothing merged and *Approve the new head & merge* as the way out. The option is also offered at 085 *Needs human decision* when the row wears needs-rebase. The fixer router now classifies a conflicting PR before skipping rows parked at needs-decision and routes a PR with an armed follow-through as pushable, so a parked PR that falls behind is no longer owned by no lane; and the unpark sweep dismisses any recorded changes-requested whose source is the hive's own `/hive decision` comment ([#15103](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15103), [#14822](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14822)).
- No merge lane ever rebased a conflicting PR — the self and trusted-author lanes stopped at prow's `needs-rebase` label before their update-branch call, and the fixer router only listed hive-authored or adopted PRs as rebase work, so PRs the operator opened under their own identity through session agents sat `dirty` with nobody kicked. Now a `behind` PR gets update-branch from its lane despite the stale label, and a `dirty` PR by the hive or a trusted author is dispatched to the fixer as a rebase-resolve task (rebase onto the base, resolve, force-with-lease to the PR's own branch) with a loop guard of two attempts per conflict episode: the Activity Monitor pill reads **Rebasing · attempt n/2**, then **Rebase failed · 2/2 — needs a person**, and *Rebase, then merge as maintainer* rides the same task; the conflict-aware merge train's rebase hook ([#15117](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15117)) now opens that same episode (`requested_by: merge-train`), and its rebase-exhausted row offers *Rebase, then merge as maintainer* live instead of disabled ([#15103](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15103)).
- **Approve** on a PR that is behind or conflicting no longer says "the merge gate takes it from here" (the gate skips a conflicting PR for good): the approval is recorded, the fixer's rebase episode opens in the operator's name, the toast reads *Approved — #N conflicts with base; hive will rebase it next pass (attempt 1/2)* with a **Rebase, then merge as maintainer** button, and the row's *Approving…* cue clears as soon as the decision records instead of lingering beside the ✓ ([#15103](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15103)).
