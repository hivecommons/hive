- A direction on a contributor's PR that the hive adopted in place now goes to the hive's own fixer instead of back to the contributor. On projectbluefin/utah-packages#1309 "Let hive decide" chose "hive's fixer: make CI green", but the relay then told the contributor their hive was nudged and moved the PR to `stage/072-returned-to-contributor`. While the hive still owns the branch and can push it, a dashboard direction or a `/hive decision <text>` comment now keeps the PR at `stage/074-adopted-by-hive`, so the review fix gate still hands the hive's fixer the instruction. The reply no longer mentions the contributor, the delegate's comment says "told the hive's fixer what to change" and "the hive's fixer pushes a new commit", the dashboard's direction toast says the hive's fixer pushes the change, and the `/hive help` reply and the status comment's "Your move" section on such a PR say its fixer takes `/hive decision` directions instead of "the hive does not push to a PR it did not open". The relay and the dashboard's delegate now share one rule for whether the hive can push an adopted branch, and the delegate names the hive's fixer only when the relay confirms the direction goes to it. Nothing changes for a PR the hive cannot push (fork-rehome-refused, no maintainer edits, a workflow-path fork, a fixer-reported capability blocker), one whose adoption escalation a decision does not release, a head the contributor pushed since, a supersession, an un-adopted clanker PR, a `/hive decision` comment on a PR still wearing `needs-human`, or the hive's own PRs.
