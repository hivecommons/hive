- Adoption close-first now asks the write-back gate before it closes, labels or reopens a contributor's original PR ([#15272](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15272)). These writes land on a PR the hive did not author, but they were only counted in the direct-write ratchet baseline, so they ignored a repo pause, the per-repo mode and the `contributor_adopt` kind policy (default L6 + opt-in label). When the gate refuses, the original stays open and closes when the hive's copy merges, and the refusal shows up in the write-back drill-down. A reopen that is due waits for the next poll, for example until an operator lifts a pause; a refusal for any other reason (the kind policy changed after the hive closed the PR) is logged at warn, because it leaves a hive-closed PR shut until a person reopens it. The ratchet baseline drops `automerge_adoption_close_first.go` (7 → 0).
