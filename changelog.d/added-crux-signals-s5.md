- crux: turn signals, the fifth work service of the Crux work-services RFC ([hivecommons/crux#114](https://github.com/hivecommons/crux/issues/114) S5). A contained run now keeps a small closed set of signals at turn boundaries — `started`, `thinking`, `tool`, `blocked`, `needs-input`, `input`, `done` — in `<workdir>/runs/<id>/signals.jsonl`: the runner writes `started`/`done` itself, a gate opened with `--run <id>` writes `needs-input`/`input`/`blocked` as it opens, is answered or expires, and the run's owner may emit the rest with `POST /v1/runs/{id}/signals` or `crux --server … signal emit <run> <kind>`. Read them paged with `GET /v1/runs/{id}/signals?since_seq=&limit=` or `crux signal list`, or live as `run.signal` on `/v1/events`. Each agent's last `KickOutcome` is now persisted under `<workdir>/signals/agents/` and served by `GET /v1/agents/{name}/kick-outcome` with `settled_for_last_kick`, so "no turn since the kick" and "the turn ended, here is how" stay distinguishable across a `crux serve` restart.
