- The Cost chart and per-day rollup no longer discard real spend revealed after a sampling gap. The discontinuity guard judged every increase of the cumulative estimate as if it had arrived at the 5-minute cadence, so when the collector had not sampled for hours or days (it only ran while a browser was polling) the single step that revealed that whole period was thrown away as a "correction" — on one spoke $10,679 of the $16,957 recorded increments, leaving a 14-day chart and run-rate that bore no relation to the headline. A step above the fixed ceiling is now kept when 20× the hive's average spend rate over the elapsed gap explains it (server `costFoldDailyRollups` and the UI's bucket code apply the same rule), and a step that is discarded now drops its token deltas too, so a day can no longer show 828M tokens at $0. Fixes [#13482](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13482).
