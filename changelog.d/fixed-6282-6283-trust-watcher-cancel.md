- The per-agent blocking-prompt watcher (folder trust, codex update) now stops as soon as its launch context is cancelled, even when a poll tick was already queued ([#6282](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/6282), [#6283](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/6283)). Previously Go's select could pick the queued tick over the cancelled context and the watcher would answer the same prompt a second time on the way out; in CI that second answer double-closed a test channel and panicked the whole `pkg/agent` shard with `close of closed channel` on unrelated PRs. The hermetic test now owns that channel with a `sync.Once`, guards its key log with a mutex, and waits for the watcher to exit before asserting, so a regression shows up as a failed assertion rather than a process-wide panic.
