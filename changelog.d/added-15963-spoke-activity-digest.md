- Spokes now publish the bounded activity digest to the hub: each heartbeat carries it, built in process from the stage tracker, with up to `hub.activity.max_items` items (default 100, hub ceiling 250). When the cap applies, the most urgent items are kept and `truncated` reports how many were left out. Private-repository items are left out unless `hub.activity.share_private` is set, but totals still count them. Once the hub acknowledges an etag, later beats send only that etag. A tracker change sends an activity-only update on the heartbeat endpoint at most once every 15 seconds, and changes that arrive meanwhile are merged into it. `hub.activity.enabled` defaults on for hosted spokes and stays opt-in for self-hosted ones ([#15963](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15963), RFC [#15861](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15861)).
