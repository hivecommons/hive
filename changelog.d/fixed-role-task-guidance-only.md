- **A contributor acting as an agent role now gets that role's guidance and their one assigned task, not the resident agent's whole kick.** A role-delegated task (and donated standby work) used to carry the resident agent's full manual kick: the hive's work list, the merge-eligible and CI-failing lists, the merge-sweep and sub-agent dispatch steps, the App-token instructions, the red-main notice and "Begin now: pick the authorized repo …". The assigned issue sat at the very bottom. On the projectbluefin hive, a contributor acting as scanner followed that template on one issue for over 100 minutes and 1.1M input tokens: it named its branch `scanner/fix-1614`, force-pushed, and polled CI for 30 minutes instead of reporting. The new `Scheduler.BuildAgentGuidance` renders the same templates through the same chain, but drops the resident session's sections and work lists. The prompt now says the assigned task is the whole job. Building the guidance also no longer runs a classification sweep over the cached queue on every assignment. Resident agents' kicks are byte-identical, and new golden tests pin them. See [What a contributor receives in a role](src/docs/contributor-trust-and-roles.md#what-a-contributor-receives-in-a-role).
