- Human-merge paths: when a trusted bot's pin bump failed `auto_merge.human_merge_pin_only` on its first head (for example because the upstream floating tag moved), the hive's `hold` stayed on after the bot pushed a head that qualifies, because every merge lane skips held PRs before the gate. The stage-label pass now runs the gate's pin-only check on the current head of a PR the hive held for a human-merge path. When it qualifies, the pass removes the hive's own `hold` once for that head and posts a notice; the lane checks the exception again before merging. A hold a person set, removed or re-added, a hold with another hold source, the head the gate first held, and repositories without the exception are unchanged.
