- The anonymous `/api/hub/activity/public` view is now built and marshalled at most once per 2-second window per parsed filter and shared by concurrent requests: query strings that parse to the default view (`?x=1`, `?hive=`) share its entry, filtered views live in a small bounded cache, builds are serialised so anonymous callers can drive at most one at a time, the ETag comes from the cached body so conditional requests return 304 without a rebuild, and toggling a hive's visibility drops the cache so a hive made private disappears at once (#16111).
