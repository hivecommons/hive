- Path globs with a `**/` prefix and a second glob character, such as `**/*luks*` or `files/**/*luks*`, now match by basename at any depth instead of silently matching nothing. The shared matcher backs `intent.guardrail_path_patterns`, `auto_merge.human_merge_paths` and `sentinel.sensitive_paths`, so those gates were disarmed for such patterns; `**/*.ext` patterns behave as before ([#15386](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15386)).
