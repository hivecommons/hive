- Add an opt-in Hub-coordinated CLI session pool for spokes. Participating Hives keep separate workspaces, UIDs, credentials, and tmux sessions; the Hub leases a global live-session slot and idle sessions are terminated after a turn. Configure `HIVE_SHARED_AGENT_POOL_ENABLED` on spokes `HIVE_SHARED_AGENT_POOL_MAX_SESSIONS` on the Hub, and optionally `HIVE_SHARED_AGENT_POOL_MAX_PER_HIVE` to cap how many slots one Hive may hold (default half the pool).
