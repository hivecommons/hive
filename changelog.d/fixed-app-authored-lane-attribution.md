- **Trusted-author tier hands App-authored PRs off under its own reason** — the sweep logged `reason=contributor-lane:app-authored tier=trusted-author` and recorded a trusted-author stage observation carrying the contributor lane's reason, which read as if the contributor lane had routed the App's own PRs. The tier now skips them as `trusted-author:app-authored`; `contributor-lane:app-authored` is emitted by the contributor lane only. Stage derivation treats both as no-opinion hand-offs, so the self-authored lane's `review-gate:no-review-bundle` keeps owning the stage; merge gating is unchanged ([#14482](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14482)).
