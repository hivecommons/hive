- A self-upgrade that is deferred while agent kicks drain (`hub.upgrade_drain_timeout_s`) is no longer shown as a stuck upgrade: the spoke reports its drain deadline in the heartbeat and the hub holds the stuck-upgrade alert, drift signal, orphan sweep, stale-upgrade re-arm loop and the dashboard's red elapsed counter until it passes. The honoured deadline is capped relative to the upgrade's start, so a spoke cannot renew it indefinitely. Spokes that do not report it behave as before.
