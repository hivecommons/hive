- **workflow models:** ingesting a repository now proposes its *intake half* alongside its pipelines: issue templates become `requested-<slug>` intake states and events, a triage label (from templates, `settings.yml`, `labels.yml`, `labeler.yml` or `CONTRIBUTING.md`) becomes a `triage` state and gate, PR-template checklist items become manual checks, `CODEOWNERS`/Prow `OWNERS` become `approver`/`reviewer` roles owning the review gate, and the default branch's protection or rulesets (fetched when ingesting by `repo`, or a probot `settings.yml`) become required CI checks, `review.minApprovals`, a `codeowner-review` gate and bookkeeping notes. Everything is `inferred` and cites `path:line` or the API endpoint; ingesting `cncf/techdocs` now proposes its triage state. Trees without these inputs produce the byte-identical model as before; `workflow-survey` counts the proposals per repository ([#17620](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/17620))
