- Push and on-call escalations no longer lose pages to provider limits or throttling ([#9170](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/9170)). Pushover titles/messages are truncated to 250/1024 characters (an oversize link is left off), the PagerDuty summary to 1024, and the ntfy body to 4096 bytes with the link kept intact — previously an oversize `Governor budget exhausted` page drew a 400, was re-sent unchanged, and was dropped. A 429, 408, 5xx, or transport failure is now re-attempted twice with a short backoff that honours `Retry-After` (capped at 30s), while any other 4xx is final on the first answer instead of burning an identical retry. The ntfy `Title` header is RFC 2047-encoded so titles carrying `—` or other non-ASCII text arrive intact, `escalation_drop_oldest` now names the event actually evicted from the full queue, and `escalation_delivery_failed` no longer embeds the request URL — an ntfy topic URL is the topic's only credential.
