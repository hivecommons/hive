- Reviewer agents no longer report a secret scrubber's placeholder as a code defect. A review whose evidence quotes masked text — `[REDACTED]`, `<redacted>`, or a bare `******` — is refused by the review relay instead of published, and the reviewer prompts and policies now say that a mask is a hive artifact to re-read from the repository, never a defect in the change under review ([#8067](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/8067)).
