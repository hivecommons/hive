- Crux module API: `crux.Process` is now its own struct (a snapshot `crux/agent` copies out of its internal agent record) instead of an alias of `agent.AgentProcess`, and the `crux` root package no longer imports `crux/agent`. `ProjectContext`, `RingBuffer`, the Copilot-token constants, `GitHubTokenLogin`, `DefaultAgentMode` and `AgentAvailableAtACMMLevel` are defined in `crux`; `crux.FromManager` is now `agent.AsRuntime`, and `SanitizePaneText`, `ClassifyAuthPane`, `AuthPaneState` and `PaneShowsBlockingPrompt` moved to the new `crux/paneclass` leaf. This is the prerequisite for folding `crux/types` into the module root ([#13963](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13963), [#13536](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13536)).
