- The contribution tree in `hivectl hives tree`, `hivectl hives web` and `hivectl tui --hives` now carries a runtime overlay from two separate local sources: **live** readiness, acknowledgement and current task from the managed relay's status snapshot (trusted only when it belongs to the relay named by the PID record and that relay is still running), and **durable** per-repository history from the local activity store through a pluggable `DurableActivitySource`. Each source is reported on its own line with a reason when unavailable, and rows keep *unavailable* instead of reading idle, connected or zero without evidence. The `hives tree` JSON/YAML output gains a `runtime` block and per-row `history`/`activity` fields. Refs #13153.
