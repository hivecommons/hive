- Escalation email no longer loses the daily digest or queued escalations ([#9167](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/9167)). A digest the SMTP relay rejects is now kept for the next send and audited as `escalation_digest_failed` instead of vanishing without a log line. A hive.yaml reload that does not touch the escalation settings — e.g. flipping any dashboard Settings toggle — keeps the running dispatcher, so queued pages and the pending digest survive it; a reload that does change them carries the digest over to the new email sink and gives the old dispatcher up to 30s to deliver what it had queued. A push retry backoff never waits past that 30s: the event gets its final attempt at once instead. Anything it still cannot deliver is audited as `escalation_dropped_on_stop`.
