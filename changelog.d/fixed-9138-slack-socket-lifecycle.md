- Slack Socket Mode no longer drops commands during routine socket refreshes: on a `disconnect` envelope (including the early `warning`) the bot opens the replacement socket before closing the old one and switches without the 5 s reconnect sleep. A socket silent for 90 s (the bot pings every 30 s) is now treated as dead instead of hanging on a path that died without a FIN, and reconnect backoff is jittered by ±20% ([#9138](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/9138)).
