- The hub serves an anonymous **public-safe Fleet activity view** at `/activity/public`, backed by `GET /api/hub/activity/public` ([#15966](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15966), part of [#15861](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15861)). It is the source of the `twitch.tv/hivecommonsfleet` broadcast, and `src/deploy/stream/fleet` now points `DASHBOARD_PATH` at it. The view shows only public-repository items and events from hives the registry lists as public on github.com. It leaves out private or unknown-visibility rows, totals and drop counts that can include private items, hive owners and every per-user grant. It never reads a session or token, so a signed-in viewer's own Fleet access cannot leak onto a public stream. The signed-in `/activity` page is unchanged, and no broadcast is activated.
