- The dashboard now tells the operator when work is parked on a human: `/api/stage-tracker` carries `waiting_on_human` (`count`, `items[{repo, number, type, kind, since, who}]`; kinds `needs-decision`, `ready-for-human-merge`, `approve-needed`, `returned-to-contributor-offline`, `other-human-wait`) and each item `waiting_on_you` / `wait_kind`. A second, orange **Hive activity monitor** sidebar badge and a **Waiting on you · N** topbar pill (hidden at zero) show the count; both, `?filter=waiting-on-you` and the new **Waiting on you** toolbar chip narrow the activity monitor to those rows; a debounced in-app toast (one per ten minutes, none in kiosk mode) fires when the count rises. Hold, advisories, decided rows and contributors still inside their grace are not counted.
