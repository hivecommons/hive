- The merge-request relay (`hive-merge`) now evaluates the same contributor-lane policy as the auto-merge sweep before merging a contributor-authored PR: a verified claim tier outside `require_tiers`, an unfinished contributor soak, a head under `min_head_age`, a missing reviewer verdict, or a `hold`/sentinel/needs-decision label refuses the request terminally, surfaces the gate in the agent's result, and is audited as `merge_request_refused`. Previously an agent's merge request bypassed those gates, so an untrusted-tier contributor PR with green CI could be merged minutes after it was opened. Hive-authored PRs keep the relay's existing behaviour, and `merge-eligible.json` no longer lists PRs parked on a human decision.
