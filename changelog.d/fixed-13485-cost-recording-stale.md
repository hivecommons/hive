- The Cost card now says plainly when the token recorder has stopped capturing the hive's spend. `/api/cost` gains a `recording` block (configured vs recorded models, last priced growth, `stale` / `stale_since` / `reason` / tracking `issue`); when a configured model has no recorded tokens or the priced total has been flat for more than two days, the card shows **No priced usage recorded since <date> — configured model(s) with no recorded tokens: … The total above is frozen history; the chart and run-rate below are not live spend · see hivecommons/hive#13480**, replaces the run-rate with **run-rate unavailable**, and badges the collapsed Overview card **recording stale**. Previously the same situation showed a confident ~$0.18/day run-rate on hives that merge hundreds of PRs a week. Fixes [#13485](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13485).
