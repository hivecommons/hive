- Reconciled the last 7 divergent policy templates so the on-disk `src/policies/` mirror and the `go:embed` compiled-in fallback in `src/pkg/policies/defaults/` are byte-identical again ([#7066](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/7066)). The two copies had drifted into complementary halves — each holding guardrails the other lacked (the scanner `rejected_duplicate` re-file guard, the `hive-open-pr` App-bot requirement, the brainstorm "no setup during capture" guards, baseline-triage and finish-existing-PRs rules) — so whether an agent received a given instruction depended on which resolution path its deployment happened to take. Each template is now a semantic union that preserves every distinct piece of guidance from both copies, closing an invisible behavioural fork that no CI check protected.
