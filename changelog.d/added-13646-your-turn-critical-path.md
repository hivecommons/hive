- **Your-turn critical path on the dashboard** ([#13646](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13646), part of [#13617](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13617)). Every your-turn issue and PR now shows what it waits on, how many other your-turn items it unblocks, and the one action that moves it (merge, release hold, rebase, rerun or approve). An explicit `Depends-on: #N` line in a PR body sets the order and agents now write it when they know the order. An issue waits on the PR that closes it, and a PR whose check is red on its base waits on that check and on the red-main fix. Overlapping PRs get only a soft "may conflict with #N" hint. Rows sort by what they unblock, dependents fold under their blocker as one expandable row ("8 PRs waiting on #255 (dakota-iso)"), the red-main banner says when the fix is waiting on you, and the phone widget's YOUR TURN counts show root causes rather than every waiting item. `/api/status` exposes the new fields per row and per repository (`waits_on`, `unblocks`, `may_conflict_with`, `action`, `repos[].yourTurn`, `yourTurn`).
