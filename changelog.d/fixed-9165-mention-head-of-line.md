- One GitHub mention that cannot be delivered no longer stalls every later mention in its repo or spends its summoner's hourly budget ([#9165](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/9165)). The poller used to stop at the first mention whose thread count or kick failed (an agent that cannot be kicked, a provider backoff), and each retry used up another per-user and per-repo rate-limit token. On the third retry the mention was declined as `rate-limited`, even though the human had already seen 👀 on it. The poller now continues past a failed mention and holds the watermark at it, and the failed attempt returns its tokens. After three failed attempts the mention is declined as `kick-failed` or `thread-count-failed`. The 👀 reaction is now added only after the kick is delivered, so it no longer appears on a summon that produced no run. A reaction that fails on a locked issue is recorded as `ack=failed` and does not block delivery. Marking a mention as seen, including an Actions OIDC dispatch, no longer moves the repo's polling watermark forward past mentions that have not been polled yet.
