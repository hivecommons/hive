- The stale-red base refresh now also asks a dependency bot to rebase a red PR that the dependency-bot sweep's own red-CI hand-off parked with `needs-human`, once every failing check is green on the base tip. That hand-off asks a person for a fresh run once the cause has cleared, which is exactly what the bot's rebase gives, but its own label used to block the request, so a person had to tick Renovate's rebase box by hand ([projectbluefin/fsdk-containers#570](https://github.com/projectbluefin/fsdk-containers/pull/570) stayed parked more than 3 days with its failing checks green on main). The label counts as the hand-off's only when the sweep's ledger records that red-CI hand-off, with the label added by the sweep, for the PR's current head, and, read once right before the request, the newest `needs-human` label event is the App's own (a person who took the label off and put it back, or a failed read, means no request on that head). A `needs-human` a person added, one from any other hand-off, or one recorded for an older head still blocks the request, as do `hold` and `do-not-merge`, and the PR-only trigger never applies to such a PR. Such a PR is asked at most once per head, also across restarts, and keeps the hand-off's label and comment; no new hand-off is posted, and when the bot's new head fails again it is not asked again. Dependabot is unchanged.
