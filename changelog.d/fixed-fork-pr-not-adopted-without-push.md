- **Contributor PRs from forks the hive cannot push to are no longer adopted and parked within minutes.** The hive never moves a fork's commits into the base repository, so adopting a fork PR whose branch it cannot push to only parked it at `stage/085-needs-decision` on the next pass, 15 minutes after the author's relay went offline. Such a PR now stays at `stage/072-returned-to-contributor` with its nudge and the relay's queued `fix-pr` task, and is parked at 085 with the same maintainer ask only after the 072 dwell (24h) since the nudge on that head. In-place adoption on forks that allow maintainer edits, same-repository adoption, and explicit owner-delegated adoption are unchanged.
