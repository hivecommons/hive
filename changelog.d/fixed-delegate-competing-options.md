- "Let hive decide" no longer approves a bare "direction" on an issue that asks its owner to choose between options. Before, projectbluefin/utah#920 (carry debugging tools in the image, a `ujust` recipe, or a `-dx` flavor) planned *Approve the direction of #920 and let the hive pick it up* under `default-approve`, recording no choice, so the claiming agent would pick one itself. The delegate now reads the issue's options (the lettered options the "What to reply" notice reads, or a numbered list a `Pick one:` / "competing" / "options" lead-in introduces, from the body or the newest hive park comment). When the same text recommends one, it goes with that option and names it in the plan and in the recorded `/hive decision` (rule `recommended-option`). When it recommends none, it writes nothing and leaves the choice to a person, offering Give direction (rule `competing-options`).
