- Upstream-sync PRs on `upstream_watch` forks (labelled `upstream-sync`, on an `upstream-sync/` branch, or titled `chore: sync upstream …`) now land as merge commits instead of squashes, so the fork keeps upstream's ancestry and the next sync no longer re-conflicts; forward-merge heads with dotted versions (`sync/v1.7.11-to-v1.9.1`) are recognised too ([#11508](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/11508)).
