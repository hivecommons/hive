- Scheduled upgrades now expose their progress as data on both dashboards ([#14950](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14950), motivated by [#14856](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14856)): the hub's `/api/saas/my-hives` carries `upgradeState` (`scheduled` → `draining` → `pulling` → `rolling` → `verifying` → `done` | `missed`, with the drain deadline/countdown and the agents whose turns are holding it — "2/5 agents in turn: reviewer 38m, scanner 12m") and `upgradeHistory`; the spoke's `/api/status.upgradeProgress` derives the same state locally and the heartbeat policy gains `next_window_at` (the hive's own window, even on `stable`) plus `upgrade_history`. The hub persists the last 14 attempts and held windows per hive (`HIVE_HUB_UPGRADE_HISTORY_MAX`) under `/data/saas/upgrade-history/`, so a missed window survives a hub restart and is never silent. The spoke's drain report now includes per-agent turn elapsed (`upgrade_drain.turns`).
