One automatic re-author per issue; a second exhaustion parks at 085 with Give direction / Hand it to a person / Close as not planned. The Activity Monitor words the issue `Re-authoring · superseding #N` (remediation evidence `kick`) and the fresh PR's chip `re-authored from #N`. `decide:re-author` is gone from the decision vocabulary: a person's rewrite of a contributor PR (unsigned DCO, exhausted) is `decide:adopt` — "Adopt as hive PR"; historical `re-author` ledger entries still read.
