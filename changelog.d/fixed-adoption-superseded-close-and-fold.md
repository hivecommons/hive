New `governor.labels.stage.adoption.close_superseded: on_adopt|on_merge` (default `on_adopt`): a clanker- or hive-authored original closes in the adopting pass by policy — the gate admits the close as `superseded-agent-pr` without the `contributor_adopt` opt-in label once the copy carries its commits and sign-off; a human-authored original still needs the label, else it closes when the copy merges. The tracker marks the original `superseded_by: M` (cleared when the copy dies, omitted for in-place adoptions) and the copy's `adopted_from.state` / `lineage.from_state`; the SPA folds the original into the copy's "↳ adopted from #N" chip, whose hover says "#N is superseded and still open; it closes when this merges." or "#N is closed as superseded." — a superseded PR whose superseder is listed is never a row of its own.
