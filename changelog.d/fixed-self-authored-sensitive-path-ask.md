- Review: on a hive-authored PR, a `requires_human` or decision-requested perspective whose in-scope finding sits on a sensitive path (for example `.github/workflows/`) now stays a person's decision under `self_authored_decision_policy: resolve`. It parks at `stage/085-needs-decision`, and the comment names the finding and the reviewer's ask. Before, an ask worded without a risk-class term, such as "apply the `pr-validate.yml` hunk from the PR body", was turned into `changes_requested`. The PR then sat at `stage/070-fixing`, with `stage/stalled` coming and going, through fix attempts that could not land the workflow edit, and nobody was asked. A recorded `decision_finding` also stops the read-time verdict normalizers from turning the hold back into `changes_requested`. Asks for changes, and asks off the sensitive paths, still go to the fixer.
