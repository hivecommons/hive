- Standby matching now weighs the WORK as well as the worker ([#8137](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/8137), step S7 of [RFC #7629](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/7629)): a new owner-authored `hub.standby_item_tiers` list says which classes of item may be offered to a standby contributor and at what capability tier, and `standby.Qualifies` requires a donated configuration to clear both the lane's floor and the item's tier — a T1 configuration may take a T3 item, a T3 configuration may not take a T1 item. The list **ships empty, and empty means it is not in force at all**: the lane floor decides alone and every hive's answer is byte for byte what it was before. Entries are keyed on `(repo, label)` with duplicates a load error, an item matching no entry of a non-empty list is `unknown` and not standby-eligible, and a `T3` entry **must name the automated signal that makes it cheap to review** — the design's verifiability test for T3 eligibility, enforced at load rather than written down. The list is authoritative: Hive's classifier can propose a tier for an item from its labels, and that proposal is carried for logging and decides nothing — an item absent from the list does not become donatable because something proposed T3 for it. No surface writes the list or even names its key, held by the same scan that keeps the model floor read-only, and no surface proposes adding an item to it. See [the standby design record](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/blob/v6/src/docs/design/standby-contributors.md) and [agent configuration](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/blob/v6/src/docs/agent-configuration.md#standby-contributors-standby).
