- A hive-authored PR whose diff against its base is empty (an adoption whose fix already landed through another PR) is now closed by the hive as superseded by the PR the reviewer named, through the writeback gate, instead of parking at "needs decision"; a person's empty PR still gets the prompt. Adoptions' dispatch records now carry `hive_authored`, so their decisions route through the self lane rather than the contributor lane. A reviewer decision question re-asked on a new head (including a merge-from-base, which no longer triggers a review round for an empty PR) is identified by its `hive-redundant-of` PR or normalised wording and refreshes the standing review in place with no second 085 comment. The tracker's decision popover quotes the reviewer's question from the newest verdict on any head, shows a "redundant of #N" chip and leads with "Close as superseded" ([#15499](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/pull/15499)).
