- Every `?kiosk=1` view — the spoke Activity Monitor, the Matrix view and the hub's public Fleet activity page, i.e. the three Twitch streams — shows a small "Request a Hive" call-to-action pill bottom-right with `hive.hivecommons.dev/get-started` and a QR code of it ([#16236](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/16236)). It never appears in the operator dashboard, `&cta=off` removes it, and the text is `dashboard.kiosk_cta_url` / `dashboard.kiosk_cta_text` on a spoke or `HIVE_HUB_KIOSK_CTA_URL` / `HIVE_HUB_KIOSK_CTA_TEXT` on the hub. `src/scripts/twitch-channel-cta.sh` sets the matching title, category and tags on the channels (`--dry-run`, idempotent, per-channel `channel:manage:broadcast` token).
