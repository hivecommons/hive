- **Tracker: "Let hive decide" for items parked at *Needs human decision*.** The *Decide…* submenu on a parked issue or PR row (and the selection bar's *Decide…* for a bulk run) now opens with **Let hive decide**: the hive makes the decision itself as the owner's delegate, under the owner's standing operating rules (`pkg/delegatedecision.PolicyText` — decisive, reversible, keep work moving; prefer contributor PRs over hive duplicates; approve small low-risk changes and trusted-author sentinel alerts with a "reviewed, intended" line; supersede offline contributors now; dedupe aggressively; secrets/PAT/org-permission items are owner-only; quarantine flaky tests; accept CI hygiene gates as ratchets and design proposals with explicit defaults; never auto-close review-backlog items), and records it exactly as a human decision is recorded — `/hive decision <text>` comment, park labels lifted, `triage/accepted`, queue approval on a released PR head — so the item leaves 085/045 without the owner. Every delegated comment carries `<!-- hive:delegated-decision by=<hive-id> policy=owner-delegate -->` and the Decisions log marks the row *delegated*, so delegated and human decisions can be told apart and counted. Rails: the delegate never merges, refuses items wearing `hold`, never grants membership/secrets/permissions (`needs-owner` + `hold` with the exact owner action instead), and only *recommends* on policy or scope changes to the hive itself (keeps 085). New endpoint `POST`/`GET /api/repos/{owner}/{repo}/items/{number}/decide-delegated`; new setting `decisions.delegate.enabled` (default true) with a per-repo override. This release's decider is rules-first (the mechanically decidable rules above; the reversible default otherwise) — the LLM-backed decider for the rest is a follow-up.
