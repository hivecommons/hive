- Approve no longer deadlocks a contributor PR the hive cannot merge as-is: when the approved head is on a fork the App cannot update and sits behind the base (`fork-not-editable`), conflicts, or lacks a sign-off, the admission no longer records `not adopted: a maintainer approved this head; the hive merges it as-is` — the hive adopts and carries the approval to its copy. Adopt now always wins over an earlier Approve: recording it clears the admission blocker at once, the row immediately reads *adopting… (queued)* with the Adopt now action gone from the menu and popover, and the decision response says `adoption-queued` instead of promising the running pass when the copy has not opened yet.
