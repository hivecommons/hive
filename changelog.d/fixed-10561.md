- **Dashboard: Governor strip no longer claims a second "actionable" count.** The Governor strip's `actionable` / `PRs` tiles showed the governor's mode-pressure inputs (every non-held issue plus every open PR, including draft, blocked, and needs-human PRs), so their sum disagreed with the Overview **Actionable now (issues and PRs)** KPI. The tiles are now labelled `issue queue` and `open PRs` with tooltips explaining the difference, and the single definition of "actionable now" is documented in the dashboard glossary ([#10561](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/10561)).
