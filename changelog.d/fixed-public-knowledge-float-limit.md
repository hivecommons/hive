- Public knowledge MCP: `knowledge_search` and `knowledge_toc` accept a `limit` written as a whole float, such as `5.0` or `1e1`. Their schemas declare `limit` an integer, which a whole float is, and clients whose JSON stack writes every number as a float send that form; the endpoint refused the whole call with "invalid arguments". A fractional or quoted `limit` is still refused, and a whole number above the maximum is still clamped to it.
