- `ioscan` canary egress detection now covers the encodings the first pass at [#6686](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/6686) left open: line-wrapped and nested base64, the URL-safe base64 alphabet, hex written with per-byte separators or `\x`/`0x` escapes, splitting on punctuation rather than whitespace, homoglyph and zero-width substitution, and partial leaks carrying an intact 16-hex-character window of a token. Candidate blobs are no longer capped per body, so padding an outbound write with decoy blobs can no longer push the real payload out of the scan, and the rewritten scan is roughly four times faster than the substring version it replaces on a full-size (2 MiB) body. `security-self-assessment.md` and `ioscan.md` now state what the egress canary check does and does not cover, replacing the stated encoding limitation.
