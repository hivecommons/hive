- The `min_head_age` merge gate no longer stalls green merges for an hour when GitHub's server-generated timestamps (`created_at`/`updated_at`/`pushed_at`) run ahead of the host clock. A head time in the future is treated as clock skew: instead of clamping to "head pushed 0s ago" for the whole skew, the automerge sweep and the `hive-merge` CI gate measure the hold from the host-clock time they first observed the head (`pending: head time ahead of host clock; head first seen … ago (< min_head_age)`), so slow check-runs are still held for one `min_head_age` window and the hold then clears. Heads whose age is zero or positive still wait as before ([#16143](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/16143)).
