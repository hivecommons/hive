- `hivectl hives hub add|list|show|remove` tracks hubs in a new optional `hubs:` key of `profiles.yml` and reads each hub's federation registry (`GET /api/hives`) to list its advertised Hives. `hub adopt` confirms and bulk-registers only missing Hives through the existing `hives add` path, `hub follow` stores a future-enrollment policy without changing current membership, and `hub refresh` reports additions/removals while retaining successful partial progress. No credentials are stored on hubs; redirects, error statuses and malformed registries report unavailability instead of empty results (#13174, #13175).
