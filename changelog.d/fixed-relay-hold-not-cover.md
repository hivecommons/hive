- Contributor-first lanes are no longer left unworked by a relay that is connected but not taking work. A relay in standby mode, or one whose quota guard or provider quota hold is holding all work, used to count as covering its role, so the governor withheld the resident agent's kicks while nobody worked the lane (seen on 2026-11-08: a scanner relay held at 15% of its weekly window kept scanner "covered"). The hub now skips standby relays, and relays tell a hub that advertises the new `quota_hold_v1` capability when a quota hold starts and ends; older relays and older hubs behave as before.
- Switching `contributor_first` on for a lane a relay already covers now logs "contributor relay covering a contributor-first lane — withholding the resident agent's kicks" once. The line was written only when the governor first saw the cover, and it records cover for every role whether or not the flag is set, so the usual order (relay connected, then the owner flips the General-tab toggle) left no log line at all.
