- Conflict-aware automerge merge train ([#15117](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15117), #15141): in every automerge lane (hive self-authored, trusted-author, contributor) approved PRs to one base whose changed files overlap are grouped per repo, and only one front per group (a head not last seen conflicting, red or held first, then the oldest) merges per sweep pass, so parallel approvals no longer dirty each other. Siblings wait at stage/080 as `merge_gate.state=queued-behind` ("In merge train · 3rd of 4", never a human stage); after the front merges the lane updates `behind` siblings and asks the rebase-resolve hook for `dirty` ones (`auto_merge.merge_train.max_rebase_attempts`, default 2; past it: stage/085 "Needs human decision · rebase failed twice" with "Rebase, then merge as maintainer"). The Activity Monitor renders a train contiguously with a left rail and a `⛓ merge train · N PRs · front #X` header chip, and `/api/stage-tracker` items carry `merge_train{id,state,front,position,size,overlap_files,ahead}`. `auto_merge.merge_train.enabled` (default `true`) turns it off.
