- When the hive adopts a contributor PR that was returned for the review swarm's requested changes (or another review ask) with CI green, the adoption note now says what the nudge asked for. It used to say "CI stayed red for 2h after the nudge" whatever the nudge was for, the superseding PR body said "adopted after CI went red", and the in-place note promised to "fix CI here". The note now reads the nudge's own diagnosis: red checks, review asks, or both. An in-place adoption held by a blocking review verdict also says that the in-place fix repairs CI only, and that a finding still open after the stage dwell moves to a superseding PR.
