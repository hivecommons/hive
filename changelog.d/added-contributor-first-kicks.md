- Contributor-first lanes: `agents.<name>.contributor_first: true` makes a connected contributor relay acting as that agent's role the lane's primary worker. While such a relay is connected (and not stale or failure-paused) the governor withholds the resident agent's kicks, so the hive's own inference budget is not spent on work a contributor is already covering; the resident agent resumes on its normal cadence once no relay has covered the role for `contributor_first_grace` (default 10m). The Governor section shows which lanes are covered and by whom (`covered_lanes` on `/api/status`). Budget pause and standby dispatch are unchanged: a covered lane is never reported as paused for budget.
