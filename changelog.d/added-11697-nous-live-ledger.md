- The Experiment Lab now reads the Nous ledger and principles live instead of a snapshot taken at boot: every `GET /api/nous/status`, `/ledger` and `/principles` re-checks `<nous_dir>/governor/ledger.json` and `principles.json` (one stat per file; re-parsed only when changed), so a round the experimentation agent finishes shows up on the next panel poll without a restart. The `type`/`status` mismatch between what Nous writes and what the panel grouped on is resolved with one shared schema (`pkg/nousstore`): every ledger entry served carries `type` (`baseline`, `experiment`, `dry_run`, `analysis`, `shadow_analysis`) and `status` (`pending`, `active`, `completed`, `aborted`), plus `outcome` from Nous's `h_main_result`; every principle carries `type` (`domain`/`meta`, from Nous's `category`) and `status` (`active`/`updated`/`pruned`). Files written without the fields load with defaults; explicit unknown values are logged and defaulted rather than hiding the row; a half-written file keeps the last good copy. The panel shows type and status chips on each principle and on a new recent-rounds list, and the history timeline is keyed by status. `/api/nous/*` paths and `NousState` are unchanged.
