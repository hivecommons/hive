- **The Hive activity monitor can play a short "bug zapper" when a pull request merges while the page is open** ([#13275](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13275)). A speaker chip at the right end of the monitor's toolbar (🔇/🔊, off by default, remembered per browser under `hive.tracker.sound`) opts in; turning it on plays the zap once. The sound is synthesised in the browser with the Web Audio API — a third of a second of crackling noise swept down through a bandpass filter over a low thunk, peak gain 0.3 — so there is no audio asset and no request the dashboard's CSP would have to allow. It fires when a PR the tab was already showing turns merged between two tracker refreshes, once per PR, never on first load, never for issues or already-merged rows, at most two zaps per refresh, and never while the tab is hidden. To make "merged" distinguishable from "closed" (the tracker lists open work only), `GET /api/stage-tracker` now also carries `recently_merged` — the PRs whose `pr_merged` audit entry (sweep, queue, relay, or a human merge the PR poller observed) is under 30 minutes old.
