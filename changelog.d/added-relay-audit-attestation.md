- The hub accepts a contributor's **opt-in** relay audit attestation: an `audit_share` on `task_complete` / `task_failed` (rows up to the shared level, clamped to the relay's capture level) writes a `relay_task_attested` audit row cross-referencing the contributor's local record. At `commands`+ the hub stores the shared command rows in `/data/contrib-audit/<login>/<task>.jsonl` (0600, 90-day retention) rather than the 500-entry audit ring. The new `hub.contribute_audit_share_request` (default empty) lets a hive *request* a level, advertised on `auth_ok` (`audit_share_requested`) and on `/contribute` before setup. Admission never depends on it ([#13458](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13458), part of [#13441](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13441)).
