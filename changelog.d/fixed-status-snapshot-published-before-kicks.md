- The dashboard status snapshot is now published before the governor's serial kick dispatch as well as after it, so `/api/status` (and the stage tracker, agent states and `governor.nextKickAt` derived from it) reflects an evaluation within seconds instead of after every due kick has been delivered — a phase that took 10–15 minutes on hosted hives and, when a rolling upgrade landed mid-dispatch, meant the evaluation never reached the dashboard at all. Snapshots the stale-build guard refuses are now logged at Warn and counted in a new `statusStaleDrops` field on `/api/status`.
