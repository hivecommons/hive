- Cost section: the spend-over-time chart's default windows are wider and adjustable — hourly 24 h, daily 14 d (month/day labels once there are more than 7 bars), weekly 5 wk (one bar per week), monthly 5 mo (one bar per month) — with a −/+ stepper beside the range chips (hourly 6–72 by 6, daily 7–60 by 7, weekly 2–26, monthly 2–24; remembered per range). Spend-in-range and run-rate follow the stepped window. Each completed UTC day of cost snapshots is now folded into a per-day rollup kept ~2 years (`/api/cost` `daily_rollup`, `/data/cost-daily-rollup.json`) so weekly/monthly windows reach past the 30-day snapshot ring; bars with no recorded history draw hatched and the summary notes where history starts.
