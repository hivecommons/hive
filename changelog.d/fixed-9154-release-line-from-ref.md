- `tagged-release.yml` and `promote-stable.yml` on `v6` no longer carry `v5` by literal ([#9154](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/9154)). Both now serve the branch they were started from (`github.ref_name`) and refuse unless it is under `release_lines` in `.github/release-lines.yml`, via the new `src/scripts/check-release-lines.sh --is-release-line <branch>`. Previously a `workflow_dispatch` from `v6` checked out `v5`'s tip, derived with `RELEASE_LINE: v5`, and merged a release PR into `v5`, or promoted `v5`'s `candidate` to `stable`. It now fails at its first step. Nothing could ever mint `v6.0.0` either; adding `v6` to `release_lines` now enables that with no workflow edit. The rest of the release-line cut (the `docker.yml` channel rows on both branches, the default branch, and docs) is now a checklist in `src/docs/release-line-guard.md`. `release-channels.md` again says `edge` is published from `v6`, not `v5`.
