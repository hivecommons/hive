- Agent policy templates now ship identically whether a hive resolves them from disk or from the binary. The templates exist twice by necessity — `src/policies/` is read at runtime by `policies.path` deployments, and `src/pkg/policies/defaults/` is compiled in as the fallback, because `go:embed` cannot reach outside its own package or follow a symlink. Nothing enforced that the two agreed, and they had not since May 2026: `${GH_AUTH}` was added to all write-capable policies but only partially propagated, so 13 embedded templates were kicking agents with no GitHub auth recipe and 14 with no `${KNOWLEDGE}` priming. Twelve templates are reconciled here (purely additive — no prose changed), and a new drift guard in `pkg/policies` fails the build when the two copies disagree, when a template exists in only one tree, or when a prompt variable appears in one copy but not the other. Seven templates whose copies hold genuinely complementary guardrails are recorded as reviewed exceptions that the guard only permits to shrink, since a wholesale copy either way silently deletes real safety rules — verified: a naive sync removed the `rejected_duplicate` re-filing guard from both embedded scanner templates with every existing test still green.
