- A decision park that defers to another issue's decision ("Answer on #N; the same choice applies here") now takes that answer when the owner decides #N with `/hive approve` or `/hive decision`: its decision label comes off — with a note saying where it was answered unless the repo's `writeback.per_repo[<repo>].mode` is `off` — instead of the issue coming back as a second ask with the same question once #N is unparked or closed (#16699). Only a genuine deferral inherits: the body must defer to #N in so many words, #N must itself have been a decision park, and `/hive done` or a release approval on #N leaves the deferring park its own ask; the inherited un-park counts against the pass's action cap.
