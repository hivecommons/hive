- A person's release of a review verdict hold (an **Approve** review or a `/hive approve` reply) now survives clean `Merge branch 'main'` updates: merges that leave the PR's own non-merge commits and its patch against the merge-base (compared like a patch-id) unchanged. Each such merge moved the head and drew a fresh `requires_human` verdict, which re-held the PR even though its diff had not changed; the hold now counts from the first blocking verdict on the same non-merge content, in the self-merge sweep, the contributor lane, the merge-request relay and the review pipeline's your-turn decision alike. A new commit, a merge that carries conflict resolution (or whose cleanliness cannot be read), or a rewritten history still starts a new hold ([#13086](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13086)).
