- A person's release of a review verdict hold (an **Approve** review or a `/hive approve` reply) now survives `Merge branch 'main'` updates that leave the PR's own non-merge commits unchanged. Each such merge moved the head and drew a fresh `requires_human` verdict, which re-held the PR even though its diff had not changed; the hold now counts from the first blocking verdict on the same non-merge content, in the self-merge sweep, the contributor lane, the merge-request relay and the review pipeline's your-turn decision alike. A new commit or a rewritten history still starts a new hold ([#13086](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13086)).
