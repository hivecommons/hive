- The weekly owner advice "Clear the human gate on PRs" and "Unblock the human queue" are readable. They no longer say only "59 of 94 open PRs are your turn (1 INSPECT, 24 DECIDE, 34 REVIEW)" followed by five items whose notes could run to a paragraph. Each now gives the count waiting on a person, **why they wait** (grouped by reason in plain words, with where they sit, such as "24 parked until a maintainer decides (20 in utah-packages, 3 in utah, 1 in testsuite)"), a **How to help** line for the largest groups, and one short line per listed item. The raw signals moved to the end of the digest entry and fold away on the dashboard card. The API adds `why` and `help` to those recommendations.
