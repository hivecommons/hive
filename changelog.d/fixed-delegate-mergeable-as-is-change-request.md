- "Let hive decide" no longer approves a PR "as it is" over a standing change request. On projectbluefin/testsuite#1057 the delegate's mergeable-as-is rung approved a green, conflict-free fork PR while a maintainer's CHANGES_REQUESTED review stood on its head, confirming the review swarm's High finding. The delegate now reads each maintainer's latest opinionated review (what keeps GitHub's reviewDecision at CHANGES_REQUESTED) and the swarm's verdict on the exact head. A standing change request rules the rung out and becomes the fix the decision names (return to the contributor, or fix-then-merge when they are offline); a requires_human verdict on the head rules the rung out too, and the reviewer's question is answered by the release rule that says so. The ruled-out reason is recorded in the decision's ladder.
