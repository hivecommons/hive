- `hivectl hives web` is simpler: the "contribution tree" is now just "What you contribute to", a one-sentence intro (glossary and keys folded into "Help & keyboard"), short row text such as "3 of 12 on · 9 new" instead of three clauses of status, repositories as a plain checkbox with a "New"/"Excluded" badge, secondary actions (Pause, Deselect all, Exclude, Add) shown only on the active row, a one-line "Your changes" summary and a four-line plain-language save receipt, with "Manage Hives" collapsed. On a 6-Hive, 72-repository list that is about 40% fewer words on the first screen (516 to 296) and a page less than half as tall (8,080 px to 3,519 px).
