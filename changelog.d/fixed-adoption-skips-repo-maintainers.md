- Contributor adoption (stage `074` and its re-home / DCO re-home) no longer takes over a PR whose author has admin or maintain permission on the repository (backport of [#14305](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/pull/14305), [#14301](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14301)). The permission is read once per repository and author per 10 minutes; a failed read is not cached and defers, neither adopting nor releasing; an existing adoption of such an author is released, closing a still-open hive superseder with a note first.
