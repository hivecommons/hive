- Dashboard: the REPOSITORIES card now shows held PRs and held issues as pills, so a PR that is waiting on a human is no longer the one thing the card hides ([#7896](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/7896)). The card's PRs number is the repo's total open PR count, but its pills were drawn from the actionable set only — and a `hold` label (the ACMM level gate's "held for human review", a Renovate bump the escalation ledger gave up on with `needs-human`, a maintainer's own `on-hold`) moves an item out of that set. So a repo whose every open PR was held read "3 PRs" with three *issue* pills under it and no PR listed, and an operator's first read was "the dashboard is missing my PRs". The pills are now laid out in two columns under the two numbers — issue pills under **issues** (blue), PR pills under **PRs** (purple) — so position says what a pill is, and colour within a column says what state it is in: held items sit in their column in a dashed neutral tint with a ⏸ chip (⚠ when `needs-human` is also on it, since that hold only a person can clear), and the tooltip names the label that holds it, says agents will not act on it until the label is removed, and on a hive-authored PR says a `hold` there is usually the level gate the hive lifts itself. The count and its column now agree. A held PR pill never offers **Queue auto-merge**, and a held issue pill never offers **Plan** — both are out of the automated lane by definition. The held items ride on the status snapshot beside the actionable ones (`heldPrs` / `heldIssues` on each repo, with the hold list's items now carrying their GitHub URL and labels); they are kept apart from `openPrs` on purpose, so the hold gate and every automated consumer of the snapshot — the contribute queue, the merge sweep counts, `/api/prs` — are untouched. The section header's "N need human review" counter now includes held `needs-human` PRs, and the hive's own review pill is stamped onto held PRs too.
