- A contributor PR whose only red check turns out to be a shared CI incident no longer keeps a nudge that says "the hive will not merge it until the head changes". The 072 nudge goes out as soon as the PR goes red, and the scanner's `[shared-ci]` incident marker can land minutes later: on hivecommons/hive, #16993, #16995, #17000 and #17001 were nudged for `native-queue-policy` about ten minutes before the scanner deferred them to incident #17003. The stage pass already moved such a PR out of 072, but the nudge stayed as posted. Now the nudge is edited in place to say it was withdrawn, for a shared incident or for checks that are red on the base branch, and the head's nudge count is reset. When the incident closes, the PR stays deferred until the shared-CI rerun sweep has re-run its failed jobs (at most 30 minutes), so it is not handed back in the gap before the re-run. The nudge also names the checks that are red on the head where it used to list the CI gate's bare `check-failure` token.
