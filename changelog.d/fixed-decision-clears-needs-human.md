- A maintainer's `/hive approve` / `/hive decision <text>` on an issue the hive itself parked with `needs-human` now lifts that source label along with `stage/045-needs-decision`, so the stage engine derives `stage/010-actionable` instead of re-deriving 045 from the surviving `needs-human` two minutes later and silently undoing the un-park (seen on [#12984](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/12984)). The rule is one function, `stageengine.DecisionLiftsNeedsHuman`: the un-park sweep reads the label timeline and lifts `needs-human` only when the last account to add it was one of the hive's own — the maintainer's decision is the human input the hive asked for; a `needs-human` added by a person, or one whose timeline cannot be read, stays exactly as before ([#12680](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/12680)), and `hold` is never touched. The accepted reply now says which happened and, when the label stays, who added it. Same rule on label-driven repos' `/hive approve` ([#13075](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13075)).
