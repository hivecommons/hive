- `src/deploy/stream/`: a headless Twitch streamer image (Xvfb + Chromium + ffmpeg, published as `ghcr.io/hivecommons/hive-stream`) with Kustomize manifests that put the dashboard's Activity Monitor on an unattended 24/7 stream, plus a dashboard `?kiosk=1` display mode that hides the chrome, dialogs and every other section ([#15594](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15594)). Guide: `src/docs/streaming-the-activity-monitor.md`.
