- Plain inception replies and `!inception answer` now collect your answers and send them in one batch once every question is answered or skipped (or on `!inception submit`), so inception no longer moves to scaffold after the first few answers and drops the rest. `!inception skip <n>` skips a question, `!inception state` shows buffered answers as answered and skipped ones as skipped, and rejected answers now get a plain-text reply instead of the raw `HTTP 400` body.
