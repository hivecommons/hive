- With `github.notifications.attention_pilot` on, each item's `hive:status` card gains an **Attention** section (who owes what, since when, snoozed until), each repository gets one `Hive attention queue` tracking issue that is created once and then edited in place, and unresolved attention episodes get at most one reminder per `github.attention.reminder_interval` (default 24h), skipped while acknowledged or snoozed and after the recipient comments, reviews or changes a label; closing an item resolves its episodes and clears it from both.
