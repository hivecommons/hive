- Activity Monitor: a PR under repair — being fixed, waiting on its author or contributor, parked on a human decision, or with a red required check — is no longer a merge-train member. The planner skips it, the tracker drops it from the train with a muted hover note ("left the merge train while being fixed; rejoins when green"), the remaining members close ranks (count, ordinals, front), and a train left with one PR renders no header and no ordinal. Hive's own #15405 led a header as "1st: #15405" while its chevron read "Fixing" ([#15615](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15615)).
