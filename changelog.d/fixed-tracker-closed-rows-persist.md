- A closed issue or PR no longer stays on the Hive activity monitor when the confirming re-read a webhook delivery queues is refused (critical API budget, rate-limit caution window) or fails: the tracker now applies the payload's own `state` on every `issues`, `issue_comment` and `pull_request*` delivery, so a closed or merged item leaves on whichever action arrives (`labeled`, `unlabeled`, a comment — not only `closed`), its eviction is handed to the governor and remembered for replay like a re-read, and an open re-read older than the listed copy can neither rewrite the row nor un-remember a close (hivecommons/hive#14159 and #14064 sat at "Triage needed" / "Needs human decision" twenty minutes after closing, through the close, a bot comment and the stage-label sweep's unlabel). As a backstop for deliveries that never arrived, on each status publish it lists each repo's items closed since its last look every `dashboard.stage_tracker.closed_sweep_interval_s` (default `300`, one list call per repo, negative disables) and evicts any still shown.
