- The `stage/085-needs-decision` comment no longer tells people that a trusted author's PR will not merge while a reviewer's `requires_human` question is open. The trusted-author lane holds on that verdict only when the review gate enforces (`governor.review_gate.mode: enforce`, or `review.require_approval`); in `shadow` (the default) or `off` it merges the head once CI is green. On [#19421](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/pull/19421) and [#19422](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/pull/19422) the comment said "the automerge lane will not merge this unattended" and asked for an Approve review, and the lane merged both minutes later at the same head. The lane now records, once per evaluation, whether it will merge the head: only when it merged or stopped on a wait that ends in its merge (CI pending, a branch update, the merge queue or train); a conflict, unsigned squash, release hold, fence or merge refusal clears it, and a lookup error or a state it could not read that pass leaves it as it was. The comment then reads "Needs a decision, but the hive will still merge this PR", keeps the reviewer's question, and names the first `auto_merge.trusted_authors.exclude_labels` entry (`hold` by default) as the label that stops the merge, and it is edited in place once each time that changes on a head. The same-head re-park of that comment, the Activity monitor's decision popover, the PR's "Your move" status section, and the `stage/071-needs-author` note a person gets when the question on their own PR routes to them (which said "the hive will not merge it yet") say the same. This wording fix changes nothing that merges.
