- The scanner and the other CI-repair agents are no longer sent to merge the base branch into a dependency bot's branch. Every hive API path already leaves such a branch (a `renovate/` head ref or a dependency-bot author) to the bot, because Renovate stops rebasing a branch once another identity commits to it, but an agent's shared-CI triage could still say `MERGE_BASE` for a red PR that was behind, and once the 60-minute busy window passed nothing in the work list said the branch was not the hive's. One scanner kick merged main into two Renovate branches this way, and Renovate then refused to rebase one of them, leaving it stranded behind its base ([projectbluefin/utah#1471](https://github.com/projectbluefin/utah/pull/1471)). Now `${PR_LIST}` marks such a row `[dependency bot: do not push]`; the FIX-BEFORE-NEW and CI_FAILING repair queues list it under its own comment-only DEPENDENCY-BOT BRANCHES heading instead of the push list, whatever the age of its head, and it no longer holds back new work; `hive-baseline-check.sh` returns `BOT_REBASE` instead of `MERGE_BASE` for a behind dependency-bot PR; and the scanner, ci-maintainer and quality policies say `MERGE_BASE` never applies to such a branch. A dashboard Approve on a behind or conflicting dependency-bot PR no longer opens a fixer rebase that the router would never hand over: the toast now says the bot rebases its own branch and still offers "Rebase, then merge as maintainer", and an operator-requested rebase of such a branch is not routed to a fixer. App-authored and in-place adopted PRs, PRs an operator armed for rebase-then-merge, fork and busy rows, and ordinary PRs are handled as before.
