A Renovate PR (a `renovate/` branch or Renovate's footer link, whatever account runs it) with that comment from its own bot now parks at 090 Ready for human merge (`renovate-artifact-problem`) until Renovate removes the comment; no approval releases it. To hold Renovate majors for a person, label them on the Renovate side with an `auto_merge.human_merge_labels` label such as `needs-owner-merge`.
