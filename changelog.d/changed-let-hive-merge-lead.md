The automerge sweep now honours an operator's recorded queue-merge approval on an untrusted-author PR and attributes the merge to that operator. On the hive's own PR the App no longer tries to review itself (GitHub 422 "Can not approve your own pull request"): the queue note is a comment, the operator's approval is recorded in the hive's store as the plain Approve records it, and the self-authored lane merges on it; the review-request watcher downgrades such a refused review to a COMMENT instead of retrying.
