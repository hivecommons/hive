- Cost section: the bar chart, "spend in selected range" and the run-rate now all derive from the one window the range selector names — hourly = 24 one-hour bars, daily = the last 7 calendar days as weekday bars, weekly = the current Mon→Sun week as weekday bars, monthly = the current calendar month as one bar per day, custom = hour bars up to 2 days and day bars beyond. The run-rate is the window's spend over its elapsed length (per hour for hourly bars, per day otherwise) instead of a fixed trailing 6 h, so switching the selector updates the chart and both summary figures together. Previously weekly/monthly drew 12 multi-week bars across 84/360 days.
