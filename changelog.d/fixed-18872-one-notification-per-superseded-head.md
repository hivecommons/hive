- A contributor no longer gets a "this PR is back in your court" nudge followed minutes later by the hive adopting the same PR. When the contributor ladder would supersede a head at once (red, conflicting or missing DCO, author's relay registered but not connected), no nudge comment is posted. The relay still gets the fix-pr task, the adoption clock starts as before, and the adoption note is the author's one notification. A head that did get a nudge keeps its grace: *Let hive decide* answers `nudge-grace` (wait; Adopt now stays the override) and does not supersede before the shortest adoption wait has passed (#18872).
