- The Cost card's headline total now uses the same estimator as the trend chart — persisted per-day totals for days the snapshot ring no longer holds plus the positive step-to-step increments inside the ring — instead of a sum of per-"lineage" peaks that double counted the residual surviving a partial reset (a counter falling 13,964 → 5,058 added the 5,058 again, ≈$6K over on one spoke), missed spend inside a lineage that dipped and regrew, and changed every time the ring evicted a sample (the "since" date crept from Aug 8 to Aug 10 within a day of viewing). Only a step that is both abnormally large and restores the counter to a level it had already reached is treated as a data correction; a large step to a new high is spend. The "since" date — and the window the merged-PR / closed-issue denominators are counted over (`count_window_start`) — now comes from the oldest per-day total and is stable, so the headline and the per-PR / per-issue tiles name the same window. Fixes [#13481](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13481).
