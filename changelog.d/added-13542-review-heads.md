- `agents.<name>.review_heads` lets the review swarm judge every perspective with several models independently ([#13542](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13542), part 1). Each head (`{backend, model, alias, replicas}`) is materialized like `replicas` as `<base>-<alias>-<n>` agents that copy the base reviewer with their own backend and model, are stripped on save and provisioned at boot by the entrypoint; `review.reviewer_agents` naming the base admits them. With `combined_perspectives: false` each perspective is dispatched once per head to that head's least-loaded replica, as a blind, record-only kick (`hive-review --record-verdict`, nothing posted on the PR), and the relay keeps one verdict per perspective per head. A decision step reconciles the heads into one verdict per perspective (see the part 2 entry); `review.head_max_retries` (Settings → Review gate) bounds per-head re-dispatch. Hives without `review_heads` dispatch exactly as before.
