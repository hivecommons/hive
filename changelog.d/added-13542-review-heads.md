- `agents.<name>.review_heads` lets the review swarm judge every perspective with several models independently ([#13542](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13542), part 1). Each head (`{backend, model, alias, replicas}`) is materialized like `replicas` as `<base>-<alias>-<n>` agents that copy the base reviewer with their own backend and model, are stripped on save and provisioned at boot by the entrypoint; `review.reviewer_agents` naming the base admits them. With `combined_perspectives: false` each perspective is dispatched once per head to that head's least-loaded replica, as a blind, record-only kick (`hive-review --record-verdict`, nothing posted on the PR), and the relay keeps one verdict per perspective per head. Until the decision step that reconciles heads lands in part 2, the aggregate takes the strictest head's verdict per perspective and stays `incomplete` until every head has reported. `review.head_max_retries` (Settings → Review gate) is accepted and validated for part 2. Hives without `review_heads` dispatch exactly as before.
