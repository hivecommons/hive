- Kick messages no longer lose a 400-character chunk when that chunk happens to start with `-`. Kicks are typed into the agent's tmux pane as 400-rune `send-keys -l` slices; without an end-of-options `--` marker tmux read a slice beginning with a hyphen (a boundary landing just before the `-` in `onboard-ai-platform`, `hold-gated`, `[ONB-2651]`, or a markdown bullet) as a flag, rejected the command, and the slice silently vanished — the error was discarded. Observed on a live hive as a held-PR snapshot that ended mid-word and jumped into a later paragraph, on which the quality agent stood down kick after kick. `SendLiteral` now passes `--` before the text and logs a failed send at WARN instead of dropping it.
