- The Cost chart, "spend in selected range" and run-rate no longer price historical models at $0. The per-model $/token map used to turn token increments into spend was taken from the newest snapshot alone; the token summary only lists the models of the sessions currently on disk, so once a hive's live traffic was down to a single helper model every Claude/GPT model in the history priced at $0 and the chart of a ~$17K history summed to $15 (flat sparkline, "run-rate ~$0.18/day" while the headline said $21K). Each model's rate is now taken from the newest snapshot that still lists that model. Fixes [#13498](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13498).
