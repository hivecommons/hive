- Review-rule capture preferences now live in hive.yaml under `review_feedback.rules` ([#19138](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/19138)): `enabled`, `trusted_associations`, `extra_phrases` (literal phrases that also mark a comment as a rule) and `disabled_signals` (built-in signals to skip). The sweep reads them on every pass, so an edit applies without a restart. `HIVE_REVIEW_FEEDBACK_RULES` and `HIVE_REVIEW_FEEDBACK_RULE_TRUSTED_ASSOCIATIONS` still work and win when set.
