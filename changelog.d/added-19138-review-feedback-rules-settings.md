- The review-feedback rule capture now has `review_feedback.rules.*` keys in `hive.yaml` ([#19138](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/19138), part of [#19135](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/19135)): `enabled`, `trusted_associations`, `extra_phrases` (your own phrases that mark a reviewer's comment as a rule) and `disabled_signals` (built-in phrases to stop counting), plus an owner-only **Rules from review feedback** card in Settings, Features, Review that edits them in place. A change applies on the next sweep pass without a restart. The keys are additive: the `HIVE_REVIEW_FEEDBACK_RULES` and `HIVE_REVIEW_FEEDBACK_RULE_TRUSTED_ASSOCIATIONS` environment variables keep working and win when set, and the card says so. See [Reviewer-stated rules](src/docs/review-feedback-rules.md#settings).
