- **Stale contributor PRs get an idle clock (`governor.labels.stage.abandon_after`, off by default).** A contributor PR parked at `071-needs-author` or at an `adoption-stalled:` `085` used to get one note and then nothing, while it kept its linked issue locked. With the setting on, a PR with no push, comment or review from anyone but the hive for that long is flagged abandoned. Its stage, label and reason do not change. The row reads `abandoned Nd: <blocker>` and lands in the inbox's "Stale: close or take over" `CLOSE` group (`abandoned_recommended_close`, which the `review_queue` MCP also shows). The delegate recommends closing it or a person taking it over (`abandoned-stale`, recommend-only). The issue it covers stays locked but shows the withheld reason `covering_pr_stalled`. `governor.labels.stage.abandon_close_repos` lets listed repositories at ACMM L6 close such PRs after one notice and a 24h grace with no activity, no hold and no `hive/keep-open`. Both keys are on Settings → Labels and in the new admin-MCP write `governor.stage_labels`.
