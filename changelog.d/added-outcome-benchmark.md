- **Outcome benchmark report:** `hivectl evidence benchmark` (and `src/scripts/outcome-benchmark.sh YYYY-MM`) renders a reproducible Markdown report from the evidence bundles comparing Hive-mediated PRs with unmediated ones — revert rate, change-failure rate, CI failure at the merged head, Hive `changes_requested` verdict rate, median time to merge and optional cost per merged PR, with 95% Wilson intervals, `not reported` below n = 10, a `control_window` on/off cut when present, and a methodology section ([#14577](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14577)).
