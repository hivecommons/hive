- The Activity Monitor's CI and Review chevrons now show counts instead of a bare glyph or a muted tint: `CI 12/14 passed`, `CI 11/14 · 1 failing (lint)`, `CI 0/14 · queued`, `Review 3/5 perspectives`, `Review 5/5 approved`, `Review 0/5 · re-review pending (new head)`. A track in progress on the current head wears the full active fill (both may be lit at once — the counts disambiguate, and the tooltip says which one the hive is waiting on), a finished track wears the done style, a red check or changes-requested verdict wears the error colour, and only a not-yet-started track is a glyph alone. The server now reports `checks_passed/total/failed/failing` and `perspectives_done/total` on every CI/Review activity ([#16493](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/16493)).
