- A merge requested through the hive-merge relay for a repo on GitLab or Gitea/Forgejo (`project.repo_policies[].forge`) now lands through that forge's merge API, after the same merge-eligible check pinned to the reviewed head. GitHub repos merge exactly as before. Authorship detection also recognises GitLab access-token bots and Gitea/Forgejo Actions as bots ([#14113](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14113), part of [#14096](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14096)).
