- The cost history that feeds the Cost tab's sparkline, trend chart and run-rate is now sampled every 5 minutes by the hive itself instead of only when a browser polled `/api/knowledge/stats`. Spokes nobody was watching had multi-day holes (up to 296 h) that the chart drew as $0 and whose spend then landed as one step when the page was next opened. No snapshot is written before the token collector has reported anything, so startup cannot record a false $0 reset.
