- Closed two gaps that defeated `ioscan`'s unicode steganography detection entirely: fullwidth Latin characters (for example `Ｉ`, U+FF29) were not folded back to ASCII, and the soft hyphen (U+00AD) was not treated as invisible. Either one hid a directive so well that the scanner produced no finding at all. Both families are now handled — the whole fullwidth alphanumeric block folds arithmetically rather than character by character.
