- `project.repo_policies[].forge` and `forge_url` put one repo on GitLab or Gitea/Forgejo while the rest of the project stays on `project.forge` ([#14112](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14112), part of [#14096](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14096)). Both keys are validated at load. The governor's escalation writes go to each repo's own forge, and a repo-scoped kick carries the forge kind and names a non-GitHub forge in its text. Unset keys behave exactly as before.
