- On a hosted spoke the Operations tab recognises a signed-in visitor again ([#7453](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/7453)). After #7364 (correctly) stopped the owner-token fallback, `/api/contribute/me` answered `401` to everyone — the hive's own owner included — because the identity it now relies on never reached `/api/contribute/*`: the `hive-contribute` Ingress had no `auth-url`, and the hub's auth-check answered a bare `200` for public paths before resolving the user. Now the hub still answers `200` on a public path for anyone, but sets `X-Hive-User` / `X-Hive-Role` / `X-Hive-Proxy-Auth` when the caller has a hub session (a user with no grant on the hive is forwarded as `read`, which grants nothing beyond anonymous), and the `hive-contribute` Ingress carries `auth-url` and `auth-response-headers` — no `auth-signin`, so nothing redirects and anonymous access to the leaderboard and the contributor relay is unchanged. The tab's *Sign in with GitHub* link on a hub-proxied spoke now goes through a gated `/auth/return?to=<this tab>` trampoline instead of `/`, so a visitor who signs in comes back to the tab they were on rather than landing on the dashboard; self-hosted spokes keep `/` (their device-flow sign-in is the dashboard root).
