On projectbluefin/utah-packages#943 the review asked that the PR's two wontfix entries stay pending; the hive adopted the PR for those asks and pushed a commit that did exactly that, and the undo guard parked it because the push kept only 2 of the PR's 20 changed lines. The park held until the head moved, so a later approve could not release it. When the hive adopts a PR for the review swarm's requested changes (CI not red), a push the guard refuses is now taken once the reviewer approves that exact head, whether the approve comes before or after the push is checked; the park is released with no second note.
