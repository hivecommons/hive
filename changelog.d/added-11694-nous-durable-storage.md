- Nous (Strategy Lab) state is now durable: the governor ledger, learned principles, baseline snapshots and the dashboard panel configuration (mode, scope, goals, output, fast-fail, schedule, controllables) all live under `/data/nous` on the PVC — configurable with `data.nous_dir` / `HIVE_NOUS_DATA_DIR` — and survive pod restarts.
