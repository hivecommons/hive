- An approval no longer carries onto a later head on a pusher-chosen commit date alone (follow-up to hivecommons/hive#16817). The date fallback now also requires that the hive had listed that commit as the PR's head by the time of the review; the first listing of each head is kept in a new persisted record, `pr-head-sightings.json` in the stage state directory. A commit with no listing on record (for example one from before the upgrade) carries nothing by date, so a backdated content push under a later base merge cannot inherit an approval. A review on a non-merge head, and the walk over diff-preserving base merges to the approved commit, are unchanged.
