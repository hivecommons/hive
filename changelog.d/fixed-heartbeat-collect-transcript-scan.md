- Hub heartbeat collect no longer times out on hives with many accumulated Copilot sessions: `AllStatuses()` used to walk every `~/.copilot/session-state/*/events.jsonl` under the agent manager (one stat per session, ~16k sessions on NFS = 8–12 s cold), which starved the 10 s collect budget, so the hub saw only MINIMAL/LAST-GOOD stale beats and reported the hive unhealthy for stable-promotion soak. The transcript-liveness walk now runs on its own refresher goroutine with hot/cold session caching, and status snapshots read only the cached result.
