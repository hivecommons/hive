- **`/hive ack` and `/hive snooze <duration>` for attention episodes, plus the attention action contract** ([#16359](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/16359), part of [#16270](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/16270)). On a repository whose `github.attention.admitter` is this hive, the episode's responsible person or a maintainer (`write`/`maintain`/`admin`) can acknowledge an attention episode or pause its reminders (1m–30d) from a comment; the un-park sweep records who and when on the episode (`acked_by`, `snoozed_by`, `snoozed_until`), refreshes the attention status section and replies once. Anyone else is ignored and nothing is recorded. Acknowledgement is not approval: neither command changes a label, releases a security/merge/review hold or opens a merge gate. `attention.Event` / `attention.NewObservation` define the contract every attention event must satisfy — who, what changed and why now, requested action, evidence, what Hive tried, what happens next, and how to ack, snooze or resolve — and refuse incomplete events. See `src/docs/maintainer-commands.md` and `src/docs/notifications.md`.
