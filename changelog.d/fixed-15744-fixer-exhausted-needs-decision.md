- Hive-authored PRs whose review fix dispatch is refused with `fix-attempts-exhausted` now move from `stage/070-fixing` to `stage/085-needs-decision` (reason `fixer-exhausted:<N>`) with a `fixer gave up after N attempts — <reviewer finding>` caption, a once-per-head decision comment and **Re-author** as the tracker's lead action; a 070 row no longer claims `Fixing · @bot` while no fix job is dispatched, a stale answered decision no longer clears `stage/stalled` while the fixer is excluded and the head is unchanged, and the `review fix dispatch excluded` warning is logged once per PR head instead of every cycle ([#15744](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15744)).
