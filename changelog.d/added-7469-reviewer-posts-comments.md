- Added `review.post_comments`, which tells review-swarm reviewers to publish their verdict as a PR comment through the `hive-review` relay in addition to returning the JSON aggregate. Off by default. On a hive without auto-merge the aggregate has no consumer, so the review was performed and then discarded; this is what makes it reach the human who has to decide. Comment-only by construction — approve, request-changes, merge, close and label are never offered.
