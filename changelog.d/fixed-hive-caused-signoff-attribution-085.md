- A PR whose sign-off attribution problem comes from the hive's own commits (the contributor's self-signed commits beside the hive's App-signed fix commit, or a head the hive pushed) is no longer returned to the contributor at 071/072 with instructions to re-sign commits they never made. It parks at `stage/085-needs-decision` with one maintainer note per head naming what clears it, and when a trusted merger approves the current head the lane elects a merge commit (`merge-method/merge`) as it already did for `role/trusted` PRs. A head made only of the author's own App-signed commits still goes back to its author.
