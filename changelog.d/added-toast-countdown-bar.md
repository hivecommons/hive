- **Dashboard toasts show a countdown bar timed to their auto-dismiss.** Every timed toast — success, info, warning, an error given an explicit deadline, and an in-flight toast resolved into its result — now draws a 2px bar along its bottom edge that drains from full to empty over exactly the toast's dismiss window, so it reaches zero at the moment the toast leaves. The bar takes the toast's severity colour at reduced opacity, never shifts layout, is hidden under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and is absent on sticky (error) and persistent (spinner) toasts that wait for a click.
