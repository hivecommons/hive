- The agent permissions watcher's tick loop now takes its lifetime and scan interval as parameters ([#6174](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/pull/6174)). `StartPermissionsWatcher` is unchanged for callers and still runs for the life of the process on the constant `PermissionFixInterval`, but it now delegates to an inner `runPermissionsWatcher(ctx, logger, interval)` that returns when its context is cancelled. That replaces a package-level mutable interval and stop channel that a first cut of the lifecycle test had introduced: with globals, the test could not know when the goroutine had actually exited before restoring `WatchedHomeDirs`/`GooseLogsDir`, and the race detector caught the watcher reading those paths while cleanup rewrote them. The tests now cancel the context and join the goroutine before touching any shared seam, with no sleeps standing in for synchronization, and cover the watcher creating its directories on startup, repairing a broken mode file on a tick, and stopping cleanly.
