- **"Let hive decide": LLM step for non-mechanical cases.** With the new `decisions.delegate.llm.enabled` (default false), an item the owner-delegate rules leave at the reversible default is decided by one model turn that sees the item, its thread, sentinel findings, linked items and the owner's policy; its structured verdict is validated against the delegate's closed action set and goes through the same safety rails, and any error or invalid reply keeps the reversible default.
