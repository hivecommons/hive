- Fixed the review-request relay refusing reviews from `ADVISORY` agents that hold the `converse` capability. The relay was gated on `AuthorizePROpen` (mode-only, `CanPush()`), while the proxy grants the same PR-review write on `converse` alone — so a converse-enabled advisory agent could post a review by bypassing the audited relay but not through it. The relay now grants on capability or mode, matching the proxy. `converse` remains conversation-only: pushing, opening PRs and merging stay on the mode ladder.
