- After a restart, a PR red only on its base branch's own check is no longer sent back to its author, or adopted and superseded, before the first CI enumeration finishes. The base-red memory and index live in the process and were empty for about 25 minutes after a start, so every base read as not red: on 2026-11-18 two projectbluefin/common PRs that had sat at 072 past their grace were adopted at once for main's red E2E check, losing a maintainer's approval, and their copies stayed red for the same reason ([projectbluefin/common#1689](https://github.com/projectbluefin/common/pull/1689)). Until the memory is published, the stage pass now reads the base tip's checks itself, at most once per base every 10 minutes.
