- The contributor relay now keeps a leveled local audit stream (`off`, `lifecycle` by default, `commands`, `reasoning`) in `relay-activity.jsonl`: lifecycle events, GitHub-touching `gh`/`git` commands captured by a pane-only `PATH` shim, and the assistant turn behind each command, all redacted before they are written and never uploaded. `hivectl hives set <name> --audit-level` chooses the level per hive and `hivectl hives audit` reads it, with `--clear --level reasoning` removing only the stored reasons.
