- **"Let hive decide" no longer asks anyone to turn a fork-guarded check green.** A fork PR whose only red check refuses it because the head is in a fork ([#14273](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14273)) used to get "make CI green without widening scope", handed to the hive's fixer, back to the author, or to a maintainer to "push the fix", none of whom can turn that check green. The delegate now reads the fork guard from the park reason, the stage record or the enumeration's copy of the head, so a PR parked for another reason (an adoption that stalled on the fork) is covered too. With nothing else owed it recommends the maintainer's step (review the change, then run or admit the guarded check for this head as its message asks, or push the branch to the repository) and keeps the park; with other items owed (a conflict, a sign-off, a review finding) those go to their owner as before, and the decision names the guarded check as the maintainer's step after them. The dashboard's 085 question for a fork-guard park now points at the check's own message instead of always saying to push the branch.
