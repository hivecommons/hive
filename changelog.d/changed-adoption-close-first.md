- Supersede adoption is close-first: the hive closes the contributor's original in the same pass that opens its copy (one note naming the copy and why, `hive/superseded`), so an issue never shows two active PRs; a copy that is closed unmerged or red at two heads reopens the original (`adoption-copy-failed`, at most once per 24h). `governor.labels.stage.adoption.supersede.close_first: false` keeps the original open until the copy merges (#15135).
