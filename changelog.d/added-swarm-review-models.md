- The review swarm now honours `agents.<reviewer>.review_models` ([#13243](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13243)): every `[review-perspective:…]` kick is planned against the PR's author model, carries the same `## Independent review — run this review with <backend>/<model>` section the queue kick uses, and respects `exclude_author_model` / `exclude_author_family` / `fallback` (`skip` withholds the kick, `requires_human` holds the PR). New `review.perspective_models` pins a model per perspective. Verdicts now carry `author_model` and `review_model` even without a pool (the reviewer-accuracy summary falls back to the reviewer's launch model), and the Evidence → Accuracy card and `/api/evidence/summary` add per-author-family and same-family vs cross-family buckets (`outcomes_by_author_family`, `by_author_family`, `by_family_match`, verdict `same_family`).
