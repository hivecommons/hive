- The fleet self-report feed's diagnostics excerpt is now bounded at 4096 bytes instead of 512: the `agent-runtime` "pane content at timeout" warning logs a `head_500` attribute before `tail_500`, and at the old cap `head_500` alone (worse with multi-byte box-drawing characters) filled the budget and silently dropped `tail_500` — the pane's current state and the only field useful for diagnosing a stuck CLI — every time the warning fired. Self-reports for `agent_pane_timeout` now carry a usable excerpt ([#15311](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15311), [#14586](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14586)).
