- A person's release of a review verdict hold now sticks ([#12957](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/12957)). Previously every refresh of `review-verdicts.json` re-stamped an unchanged verdict's `recorded_at`, and the self-merge sweep only counted an approving review or `/hive approve` posted after that time, so a `requires_human` PR was held again within minutes of its owner releasing it. Each verdict now carries a `first_recorded_at` that refreshes leave alone, and the hold is judged against it; `recorded_at` still drives retention. A new head, body digest, or outcome is still a new verdict that needs a new release.
