- The My Hives row no longer shows "Switching to <channel> — taking longer than expected" forever after a channel switch has completed ([#6301](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/6301)). The spinner was the page's own click sentinel, which for a channel switch only cleared when the hive's reported branch changed; a channel image heartbeats its baked-in branch, so it never did, and six hives that were Ready on `:candidate` for twenty minutes kept spinning until a reload. The sentinel now expires on the same signal the hub uses to clear its side, the reported image tag matching the switch target. The plain "Upgrade now" sentinel has the same shape when the hub accepts the click and then clears it as a no-op because the hive is already at everything its tag can deliver: the commit never changes, so the row spun until reload. That sentinel now expires when the hub reports no upgrade in flight, no armed target, and the hive at its reachable target.
