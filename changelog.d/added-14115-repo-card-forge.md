- Projects cards on the dashboard now name their forge: each repo in `/api/status` carries `forge` (`github`, `gitlab` or `gitea`) and, for GitLab and Gitea/Forgejo, `forgeUrl`, so a hive with `project.forge: gitlab` or `gitea` shows its own host (for example `🦊 GitLab · gitlab.example.com`) and links to it instead of claiming github.com ([#14115](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14115)). The new [Forges](src/docs/integrations/forges.md) page documents what the GitLab and Gitea/Forgejo adapters support today.
