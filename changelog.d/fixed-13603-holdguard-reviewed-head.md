- The hold guard no longer re-holds a PR whose branch was rebased earlier during the hold when a human (not a Hive account or bot) removed the gate label after the current head arrived on the branch: that lift reviewed the current head, so the snapshot clears without the integrity comment or a fresh `hold`. The head is accepted only on evidence a contributor cannot forge: the server-stamped `head_ref_force_pushed` event that produced it, or the hive itself having observed that exact head on the PR while the gate was still on. Commit committer dates and SHA-keyed check-suite/status times are never used; a push after the lift, a head the hive never saw while held, a lift by the hive or a bot, or a failed lookup still re-holds as before ([#13603](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13603)).
