- Review findings the backlog linker folded into another issue no longer vanish when that issue closes: every ten minutes, alongside the closed-issue stage sweep, the hive reads the `hive:review-backlog-link` comments on recently closed backlog hosts and re-files each still-unchecked `- [ ]` finding as its own backlog issue ("Split from #N (host closed)"), comments the new link on the host, and moves the finding's ledger and fingerprint records to the new issue. Hosts closed as not planned or duplicate keep the findings that match them and only split out unrelated ones; re-runs are idempotent via the new `split_keys` ledger entry keyed on the link-marker key ([#13914](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13914), part of [#13906](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13906)).
