- The Operations page's "Your contribution" card now shows how many of your pull requests landed in the last 24 hours, next to the all-time count it already had ([#7894](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/7894)). Issues worked already came as a 24h figure and an all-time figure, but PRs produced — the number auto-promotion actually reads — only had the all-time one, so you could see that you worked 41 issues today without knowing how many of them turned into a pull request. The new tile keeps the same honesty rules as the issues tile: an em dash and "no hourly history yet" until the spoke has rolled up your PR series, and "Nh of history so far" while fewer than 24 hours back it. Under the hood `/api/contribute/me` gains `prs_produced_24h`, `pr_window_hours_covered` and `pr_history_available`, summed from a second per-contributor hourly ring (`per_user_pr` in the persisted metrics file) rolled up from each profile's verified-PR counter exactly like the completion ring, so it survives restarts the same way. On a spoke upgraded mid-day the PR tile reports the hours measured since the upgrade rather than a full day of zeros nobody measured.
