- `src/docs/env-vars.md` and `src/docs/security-model.md` now describe `HIVE_PROXY_ALLOW_PRIVATE_FORWARD` as it behaves since [#16785](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/pull/16785): the destination guard covers plain-HTTP forwards, uninspected CONNECT tunnels and transparent-TLS passthrough, and the override lifts it for all three legs at once. The docs previously described the guard and its override as plain-HTTP-only.
