- The review swarm no longer deadlocks on verdicts whose `body_digest` or `head_sha` the reviewer miscopied, or when a PR's review runs out of redispatches ([#14361](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14361)). The relay now stores every dispatched verdict under the head SHA and body digest its dispatch recorded, ignoring the reviewer's copy. Models had returned a prose summary, a `sha256:`-prefixed hash or a hash of something else. Each such verdict was filed apart from the PR's review, so its perspective never counted as delivered and its pending entry never cleared. Separately, under one review kick in flight per agent (#13794), every pending entry made its agent busy. That included entries an exhausted head deliberately keeps (so it is never re-kicked) and entries already past `pending_ttl` on a PR not yet reached this cycle. Three such PRs pinned every review head and the decision agent, and the swarm dispatched nothing for about 36 hours while every reviewer sat idle. Now only live entries (inside `pending_ttl`, on a head that is not exhausted) count as load. Pending entries naming agents the hive no longer runs (a replica or head removed by lowering `replicas`) are pruned so their perspective is asked again. An exhausted head is labelled with the `requires_human` verdict label instead of staying on `reviewing` indefinitely. Review prompts now tell reviewers to copy `head_sha` and `body_digest` verbatim or leave them out. `review.verdict_labels` fixes: a PR moving to a new state now loses its previous verdict label, even one the hive had already removed once. Before, the second removal was deduplicated as already done, so `hive-review/in-progress` and `hive-review/approved` could both show. A PR that merges or closes while carrying a verdict label now has it removed (at most 25 removals every 10 minutes).
