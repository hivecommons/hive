- **Guide's `HIVE_FLOW` line now says why it reports `clogged`.** A new `clogged_by=` field names each threshold that tripped (`surge`, `oldest`, `mttm`), so a long SURGE or one old item with `merged_sample=0` no longer reads as slow merges. Draft PRs and PRs whose review decision is `CHANGES_REQUESTED` no longer count toward `oldest_actionable`, because they wait on their author. The guide policies also tell Guide to check operator and repo knowledge for PRs held on purpose, such as release PRs, before it calls them blockers ([#20273](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/20273)).
