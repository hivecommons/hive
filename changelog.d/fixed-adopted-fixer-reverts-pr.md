- The hive no longer merges an adopted PR after its own fixer undid the PR's change. On projectbluefin/utah#1091 the adopted fixer made CI pass by re-adding the 25 `baselines/triage.toml` entries the PR removed, leaving a docs-only diff, then announced "sweep will merge when green". Each hive push on an in-place adoption is now checked: if the PR's diff against its base keeps less than half of the lines it changed before the push, the push is refused, the PR goes to `stage/085-needs-decision` (`adoption-fixer-reverts-pr`) with a "Fixing CI would undo this PR's change" note, and the contributor lane will not merge that head. Until the check passes, the lane holds the head (`contributor-lane:adoption-intent-pending`), and an agent's "sweep will merge when green" note on it is rewritten to say the check comes first. The scanner policy tells the fixer to park instead of pushing such a commit.
