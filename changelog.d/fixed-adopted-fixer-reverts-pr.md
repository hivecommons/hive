- The hive no longer merges an adopted PR after its own fixer undid the PR's change. On projectbluefin/utah#1091 the adopted fixer made CI pass by re-adding the 25 `baselines/triage.toml` entries the PR removed, leaving a docs-only diff, then announced "sweep will merge when green". Each hive push on an in-place adoption is now checked against the PR's diff at the contributor's head the adoption started from: if fewer than half of those changed lines remain, the push is refused (so is any push on top of it, and stale-red recovery never releases it), the PR goes to `stage/085-needs-decision` (`adoption-fixer-reverts-pr`) with a "Fixing CI would undo this PR's change" note, and the contributor lane will not merge that head. Until the check passes, the lane holds the head (`contributor-lane:adoption-intent-pending`), and an agent's "sweep will merge when green" note on a hive push is rewritten to say the check comes first. The scanner policy tells the fixer to park instead of pushing such a commit.
