- The self-merge sweep, the lgtm queue sweep and the `hive-merge` relay now route through the serialized lane for repos with `merge_strategy: hive-serialized` ([#10889](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/10889)). Every existing gate still applies (auto-merge switch, pause, ACMM level, holds, CI, approvals); the lane only adds to them. Only the PR at the front of the lane merges, after a final re-check that the path's own authorization still holds for the exact head. Other PRs are deferred with "not at the front of the lane", which is expected, not an error, and one sweep pass never merges two PRs into the same lane branch. A target branch with GitHub's native merge queue gets no direct merge from Hive, and the refusal says why. `direct` repos (the default) behave exactly as before.
