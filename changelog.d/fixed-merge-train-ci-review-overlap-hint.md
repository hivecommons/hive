- The Activity Monitor no longer draws a merge train over PRs that are still at CI / Review: the merge train is a merge-queue concept, so only PRs at 080 and beyond ride one and the header counts only them. A PR short of the queue whose files overlap another open PR's now shows a muted "overlaps #N" badge ("overlaps #15637 (sidebar.html, index.html) — will queue behind it") instead of a rail, ordinal or "1st:" header ([#15653](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15653)).

The train header no longer says "not in this view: 7 more" over PRs that were never in the merge queue: `size`/`members` count only the PRs riding in this payload, and when the page does not draw every member the header says why — `on another page: #N`, `hidden by the filter: #N`, `not in this payload: #N`. The header's hover explains the mechanics (members touch the same files, merge one at a time in order, each rebased onto the base only after the PR ahead merges) and a member's hover says its slot, `Position: 3rd of 4 · after #X merges`.
