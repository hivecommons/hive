- The merge train now seats only PRs whose current head is green with every required review verdict recorded, mergeable and not held; a PR short of that is a candidate without a position and never the front. A member whose head moves, whose CI goes pending or red, who owes a verdict again or who conflicts is ejected on the next evaluation (`merge train: member ejected pr=N why=…`), the next ready member fronts, and the ejected PR re-enters at the back once ready. The stage engine and the Activity Monitor keep such PRs below stage 080 — in the CI / Review column, never the Merge queue column — with a caption naming what is owed and a gray "left merge train" lineage pill. A member being rebased shows "Rebasing · attempt 1/2" in the CI / Review column; the train header counts seated members only; a seated row always names its seat rather than "Ready for auto merge · checking now", which now applies only to the PR the sweep is evaluating ([#15825](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15825)).
