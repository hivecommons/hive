- Conflict-aware merge train in every automerge lane ([#15117](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15117)): approved PRs to one base with overlapping changed files are grouped per sweep, one front merges at a time, siblings wait at stage/080 as `merge_gate.state=queued-behind` ("In merge train · 3rd of 4", never a human stage), and after the front merges the lane updates `behind` branches and asks the rebase-resolve hook for `dirty` ones (`auto_merge.merge_train.max_rebase_attempts`, default 2; past it: stage/085 "Needs human decision · rebase failed twice" with "Rebase, then merge as maintainer"). The Activity Monitor renders a train contiguously with a left rail and a `⛓ merge train · N PRs · front #X` header chip, and `/api/stage-tracker` items carry `merge_train{id,state,front,position,size,overlap_files,ahead}`. `auto_merge.merge_train.enabled` (default on) turns it off.
