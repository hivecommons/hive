- Tagged releases resume: a `release: none` HTML-comment marker that rode in a `changelog.d/` fragment (#12928, merged 2026-10-19) was compiled into `## Unreleased` on every merge and made the release workflow's decide job skip every commit since v5.186.4 as an "explicit human override" — the fragment could only be consumed by the very release it vetoed. The fragment is removed; `check-changelog-fragment.sh` and `compile-changelog.sh` now reject a `none` marker in a fragment (bump markers stay allowed); and a hand-placed `none` freeze in CHANGELOG.md is surfaced as a workflow warning annotation and fails the decide job once it has outlived `RELEASE_FREEZE_MAX_DAYS` (default 7) instead of silently skipping forever. Also fixed on the way out: with the resulting 1,369-fragment backlog the bump inference's `grep -q` died of SIGPIPE under `pipefail` and would have labelled the catch-up release a patch instead of the minor its `### Added` section calls for.
