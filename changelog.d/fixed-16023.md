- An adopted (or otherwise hive-owned) conflicting PR no longer sits on "Rebasing · attempt 1/2" while the fixer is busy: each rebase attempt is routed once and gets `governor.labels.stage.adoption.rebase_attempt_timeout` (default 15m) to push before it counts as failed and the next attempt is dispatched, the row shows the wait ("Rebasing · attempt 1/2 · waiting for scanner 12m"), and an in-place adoption whose attempts are spent re-homes to a hive-owned superseding PR at once instead of after the 3h dwell ([#16023](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/16023)).
