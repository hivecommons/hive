- **Primed knowledge is now about the task, not about the hive's own routing.** The "# Relevant Knowledge" search built its query from an issue's labels and title words, and on hive-routed work those were mostly the hive's own: `stage/010-actionable`, `agent/reviewer`, `approved-direction`, and the `[reviewer] Review backlog (style):` title stem. Those words match review and scanning notes in every repo, so a scanner task on `projectbluefin/utah-packages#879` was primed with entries about other repos. The query now leaves out the hive's routing label families (`stage/`, `agent/`, `hive/`, `triage/`, `size/`, `lifecycle/`, `do-not-merge/`, `needs-`, `dco-signoff`, `sentinel-`, plus `approved-direction`, `hive-review`, `lgtm` and `approved`) and the filer tag, and cuts a review backlog title down to its perspective (`style`, `security`) and finding. The task's repository now counts too: an entry tagged `repo:<org>/<name>` for the repo being worked on ranks ahead of an otherwise equal entry about another repo. The tag only orders entries the search already found; it never adds one on its own. This applies to resident kicks and to contributor role guidance alike.
