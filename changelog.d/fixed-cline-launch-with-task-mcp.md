- Agents on the `cline` backend are no longer refused for a connection Hive added itself. Hive adds a `hive-task` connection for its own task MCP endpoint to every agent, and the Cline policy check counted it as operator configuration, so a `backend: cline` agent with no `tools` or `connections` of its own was refused in its pane and on sandbox kicks with "Cline cannot enforce Hive tools/connections configuration". That connection no longer counts. An agent with its own `tools` or connections, or a `hive-task` connection that points anywhere else or carries its own `auth`, `env_name` or `options`, is refused as before.
