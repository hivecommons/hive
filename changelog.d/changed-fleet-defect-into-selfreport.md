- Fleet self-reporting no longer files standalone `Fleet report: … hive-code defect` issues ([#13470](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13470)). Those were synthesised from bare dashboard counters (`StallNudges`, `Restarts`, `CIPollNudges`) with no identity, owners, spoke link or diagnostics, and nobody could act on them (hivecommons/hive#13465; 12 of 18 in two weeks were closed by the bot itself). The conditions are now `runtime-error` self-report classes — `agent_stall` (≥3 consecutive unrecovered "continue" nudges, with last-kick time and frozen-pane hash), `agent_crash_loop` (the watchdog's crash-loop latch, acting mode only) and `ci_poll_nudge` (one per 24h window) — observed by the agent manager and watchdog at the moment they happen, with a scrubbed pane tail as diagnostics. Every remaining report (`acmm-shortfall` included) now carries the hive name, spoke link, owners, repos and `Affected hives` block and uses the self-report label set; an ACMM shortfall about a component this hive already has an open self-report for posts a short roll-up comment on that issue instead of filing its own. The hub relay's label-rejection retry falls back to the core self-report labels, never to none.
