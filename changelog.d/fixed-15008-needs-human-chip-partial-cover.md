- The Hive activity monitor's **Needs human** chip now counts and filters only rows a person alone can advance — stages 015/045/085/090, 071/072 with a human author, or a `needs-human` / `needs-decision` / `unauthorized` pill — from one predicate shared by the chip count, the filter, and the new `needs_human` / `on_hold` payload totals; held rows get their own **On hold** chip, and `hold`, `stalled`, `blocked`, handled regressions, covered 040 issues and auto-mergeable PRs no longer appear under Needs human. A hive PR that only references an issue (`Refs #N`, no closing keyword) is recorded as *addressing part of* the issue instead of *covering* it, in the relay, the claim-ledger backfill and the stage reason/caption; a bare cross-reference such as a shared-CI incident deferral never creates a covering relation ([#15008](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15008)).
