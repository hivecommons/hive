- Nous (Experiment Lab) gates now reach the owner and the answer reaches the agent. The agent-side bridge (`bin/nous-hive-gate.py`) used to *PUT* its question to the owner-only `/api/nous/gate-decision` endpoint, which always failed, so nothing ever showed in `GET /api/nous/gate-pending` and every design/findings gate silently timed out as a reject. The bridge now publishes `<nous_dir>/gates/pending.json`; the hive mirrors it into the Experiment Lab (polling every `nous.gate_poll_seconds` / `HIVE_NOUS_GATE_POLL_INTERVAL`, default 15s, and on every `gate-pending` read), pings the owner once per gate, and `PUT /api/nous/gate-decision {"decision":"approve|reject|abort","reason":"…"}` records `gates/decision.json` + `gates/history.jsonl` for the agent to pick up. Decisions are validated (`400` for an unknown value, `409` when nothing is pending or `gate_id` is stale) and audited with the gate id. RFC [#11585](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/11585) part 3/8 ([#11696](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/11696)).
