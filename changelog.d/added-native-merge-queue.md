- Automerge sweeps enqueue eligible PRs into GitHub's native merge queue with the validated head pinned, report queue position and state, and withdraw Hive-enqueued entries only when the PR loses merge eligibility (a hold, a withdrawn or stale approval, changes requested, a paused or disabled repo, or a draft or closed PR) while preserving entries queued by people. On a queue-enabled base a `blocked` or `behind` PR is enqueued rather than branch-updated, and the default-on current-base gate defers to the queue's merge group. A mixed-authorship PR whose queue uses `SQUASH` parks at `stage/085-needs-decision` with the `merge-method` explanation, and the refusal is remembered per head for 30 minutes.
