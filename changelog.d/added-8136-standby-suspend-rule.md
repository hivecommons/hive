- The standby-contributor suspend rule from [RFC #7629](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/7629) now exists as a pure function over a donated-PR outcome ledger, `pkg/standby/suspend.go` ([#8136](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/8136)): N consecutive closed-unmerged donated PRs (default 2) suspend that contributor-plus-configuration tuple on that hive until the owner clears it, a merge in between breaks the streak, and the owner's clear is appended as a `cleared` row so the ledger stays an audit record rather than being edited. A merge that only happened because somebody other than the donor pushed to the PR is recorded as `merged_after_rework` and counts as evidence in neither direction — it neither resets the streak nor advances it — classified from the author set `holdguard` already snapshots at hold time, rather than from a second answer to "who touched this after we recorded it". Nothing calls it yet: no outcome is recorded, nothing is suspended, and behaviour on every hive is unchanged; the dispatch path that writes the ledger arrives with [#8135](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/8135). Every ambiguity in the rule resolves toward *not* suspending — an unrecognized ledger row is skipped rather than counted, an empty ledger is not suspended, and a threshold nobody configured means the documented default of 2 rather than the zero that would suspend every donor on sight. See [the standby design record](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/blob/v6/src/docs/design/standby-contributors.md#suspend-rule).
