- Hive now has the standby-contributor matching rules from [RFC #7629](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/7629) as a pure package, `pkg/standby` ([#8056](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/8056)): the `T1 > T2 > T3 > unknown` capability ordering, the whole-configuration tier map behind `hub.standby_model_tiers`, the per-lane floor comparison, and the rolling-day cap accounting that decides whether an approved contributor has a donated slot left. Nothing calls it yet — no lane counts anybody and nothing dispatches — so behaviour on every hive is unchanged; what lands is the decision itself, in one place, testable as a table. Three rules it is built to hold: a configuration the owner never mapped resolves to `unknown` and clears no floor, refused by its own explicit guard so the reason reads `configuration_unknown` rather than `below_floor`; a lane policy carrying no legal floor admits nobody rather than everybody; and a refusal states its class and never the delta, with the tier ordering unexported so no surface can render "you would qualify one tier down". The daily cap is a trailing 24-hour window rather than a calendar day, matching `tier_limits.max_per_day`, so a burst cannot straddle a reset, and it decrements at dispatch, so an abandoned donated task still costs a slot. See [the standby design record](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/blob/v6/src/docs/design/standby-contributors.md).
