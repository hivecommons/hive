- Activity Monitor display mode (`?display=1`) now says when its data is stale ([#15930](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15930), part of [#15860](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15860)). A wall screen used to keep showing its last payload as if it were live after the event stream or the spoke went quiet. Now the display header shows the hive id, a clock and a live dot. When the stream has been silent for more than two poll intervals, or the payload is older than two cache TTLs, the dot turns amber, the board dims and a banner reads *Stale: last update N ago*. The display recovers by itself when updates resume. After five failed reconnects it reloads on a 30 s → 10 min backoff, and it reloads once after an upgrade finishes. The URL, and so the view, survives the reload. The ordinary Activity Monitor and headless `?kiosk=1` output are unchanged.
