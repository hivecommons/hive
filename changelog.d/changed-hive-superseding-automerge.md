- Hive-authored superseding PRs (`Supersedes #N`, opened by the hive App) now auto-merge with no operator decision: a reviewer `requires_human` — security-sensitive included — is routed to the fixer instead of parking at `stage/085`, and only an unacknowledged sentinel finding, a hold, exhausted fix attempts, red CI after fixes or an unresolvable base conflict park it, each named in the 085 caption. New knob `auto_merge.hive_superseding.auto_merge` (default `true`).
