- Contributors can opt in to running the contributor container inside a krun microVM with `HIVE_CONTAINER_OCI_RUNTIME=krun` (environment or `contributor.env`): rootless podman and headless only, with a `/dev/kvm` and guest-kernel preflight, VM sizing from `HIVE_CONTAINER_MEMORY`/`HIVE_CONTAINER_CPUS`, an `Isolation: microVM (krun)` banner line, krun-aware `just contribute-stop` and `hivectl` relay controls, and no fallback to a plain container; unset leaves the launch unchanged (#12199).
