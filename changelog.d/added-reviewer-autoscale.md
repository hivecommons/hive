- `review.reviewers.autoscale` (off by default; Settings → Review Gate → Reviewers) sizes the reviewer lane to its backlog ([#14685](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14685)): the planner now reports the PRs it left waiting for a free reviewer or a parallel slot, and a controller adds a `reviewer-N` replica after `scale_up_cycles` cycles behind `target_backlog_per_reviewer` (or a wait older than the review-pending stage dwell), drains the top replica after `scale_down_cycles` idle cycles and retires it only once its turn ends. Scale-ups are refused by named guards — `at-max`, `cooldown`, `saturated` (kicks not reaching the CLI prompt), `provider-budget`, `provider-headroom`, `max-parallel-reviews`, cgroup `cpu` / `memory`. Dynamic replicas go through the ordinary replica expansion (same role, prompt, token, pane and cadence as `reviewer-2`) and the entrypoint pre-creates their users up to `max`. Each decision logs as `reviewer autoscale: 2→3 reason=backlog=7 oldest=41m0s`; `/api/status.reviewerAutoscale` and an *auto* badge on the lane's agent cards show current/target/min/max and the last decision.
