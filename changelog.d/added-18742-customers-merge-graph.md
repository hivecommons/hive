- **Customers: graph view, merge and split, export (#18742).** A profile now has a **Graph**: a drawing of the person, the things they touched and the other people on those things, read from the customer graph; clicking another person opens their profile, and a read-only user's graph holds only themselves. The owner and read-write users can **merge** a customer into another when they are the same person (identities, tags, goals and activity move across, and two dashboard users are never merged), **undo** a merge exactly (only what the merge brought goes back), and **split off** an identity, which moves it and the activity it produced to a new customer. The owner can **export** everything held about a person as a JSON download (the profile, merge history, hints and up to 10,000 activity references). Four new routes under `/api/customers/{id}`, audited as `customer_merge`, `customer_unmerge`, `customer_split` and `customer_export`; a read-only user can use none of them, including on their own profile.
