- Experiment Lab campaigns can declare pinned dependencies — repos by full commit, files by URL plus sha256 — and `hive nous-setup` (run by `bin/nous-runner.sh` before the engine starts) fetches them from allowlisted hosts only, verifies every pin, caches them and records the resolved versions plus toolchain versions in `<nous_dir>/<run-id>/dependencies.json`. The allowlist is `nous.fetch_allowlist` / `HIVE_NOUS_FETCH_ALLOWLIST` (default `github.com`, exact hostnames, deny-by-default) and is reported by `GET /api/nous/status`; a fetch outside it, an unpinned declaration or a hash/commit mismatch refuses to start the campaign. Supply-chain guards now fail CI if the Nous pins drift or the runner grows a fetch of its own. RFC [#11585](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/11585) part 7/8 ([#11700](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/11700)).
