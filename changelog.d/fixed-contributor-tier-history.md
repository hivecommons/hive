- Every contributor trust-tier change (operator trust/revoke, newcomer→contributor auto-promotion, registration) now appends `{tier, prev, at, by, reason, source}` to the contributor's `tier_history`, writes a `contributor-tier-changed` audit event, and is shown as "tier since … · by … · reason" on the Contributors view and in `GET /api/contributors`; same-tier writes are no-ops. The stage lane re-reads the author's registry tier every sweep — including for PRs parked on needs-rebase/hold/excluded labels — so a promotion or demotion reaches parked PRs on the next pass instead of sticking to the stale `role/*` label ([#15177](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15177)).
