- The v6 dashboard has a **Search** section over the portfolio search index (`GET /api/search`): a query box, kind/repo/verdict/sentinel-rule facet chips, results linking to the PR, issue or bead, a visible marker on redacted hits and a note naming repos the index could not read. Each Projects card also shows a report-only health badge (score and status, components in the tooltip) from `GET /api/repos/{owner}/{repo}/health`. Both stay hidden while `search.enabled` is off, and the Search section is hidden from viewers who are not an owner or maintainer ([#14888](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14888)).
