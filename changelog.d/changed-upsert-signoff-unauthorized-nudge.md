- Hive no longer posts a new stage-072 contributor nudge on every push to a red contributor-agent PR ([#17067](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/17067)). On 2026-11-05 the nudge reached 34 comments a day. Each PR now keeps one `hive:contributor-nudge` comment, and its marker carries `reason=<slug>`. A new head that is red for the same reason, or a change of reason, edits that comment in place, which notifies nobody. If the same reason lasts longer than the new `auto_merge.nudge_repeat_after` setting (default `72h`), the hive posts one fresh re-nudge, then goes back to editing. Existing per-head nudge comments are adopted and rewritten in place. The relay `fix-pr` dispatch and the adoption grace are unchanged.
