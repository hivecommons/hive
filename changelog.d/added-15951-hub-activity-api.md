- Spokes can attach a bounded, read-only activity digest to their hub heartbeat, and the hub serves it per user and per hive on `GET /api/hub/activity` with ETag polling: 100 items by default under a hard 250-item server-side ceiling, private-repository items excluded unless the spoke opts in (always counted in totals), truncation and stale, not-reporting or missing hives disclosed, and digests kept in hub memory only. Nothing populates the digest yet and no rollout or participation setting changes ([#15951](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15951), RFC [#15861](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15861)).
