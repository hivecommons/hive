- Two review-aggregate paths no longer approve heads they should not. On a contributor PR, a perspective's `reject` next to another perspective's non-security decision question resolved hands-free to `approve` and merge-eligible (with a `changes_requested` too, to the fixer lane): the contributor rule made the question advisory and dropped the reject with it. The head now gets the verdict the reject alone gives it, `reject`, which holds the PR for a person; the question stays in the reasons. On a hive-authored PR, the advisory approves for a non-risk decision question and for a bare non-risk `requires_human` never checked review coverage, so a head whose security review never arrived (a dead kick, or since #16994 a rate-limited review the relay does not record) was merge-eligible. With a perspective missing the head now reads `incomplete` (`review coverage incomplete: missing security`), and the planner re-asks only that perspective under `pending_ttl` / `max_redispatch`. A fully reviewed hive PR's non-risk question is still advisory.
