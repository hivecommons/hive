- The Projects pill legend now labels every pill the same way: the glyph (when there is one) followed by an uppercase word. Icon-only legend pills such as `⚠`, `⏸`, `⑂`, `👍` and `🔀` now read `⚠ HUMAN`, `⏸ HELD`, `⑂ CONFLICT`, `👍 APPROVED` and `🔀 QUEUED`, and the lowercase band and merge-state words (`unclaimed`, `eligible`, `sentinel`, …) are uppercased to match the status chips on the cards. Card rows are unchanged ([#11718](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/11718)).
