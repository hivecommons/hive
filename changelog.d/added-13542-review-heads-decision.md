- Review heads now end in one agreed verdict ([#13542](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/13542), part 2). Once every head has answered every perspective, the base reviewer gets one decision kick per PR carrying every head's full report. It checks each head finding against the code, gives it a disposition (confirmed, not reproducible or out of scope) with a reason, sets the final severity of each confirmed finding, records one decided verdict per perspective, and posts the PR's only review comment, tagging findings with the heads that raised them. Routing (`severity.block_at`, backlog filing, fix kicks, human holds) runs on the decided reports; the heads' raw reports never route on their own. When every head approved with no findings, no decision session runs and, with `acknowledge_no_findings` on, the hive posts a single acknowledgement. `review.head_max_retries` is now enforced per head: a head that never answers is named in the decision and the comment instead of stalling the PR, unless no head answered, where `max_redispatch` applies as before. `/api/reviewer/accuracy` adds `review_heads`, scoring each review model's head reports against the decision.
