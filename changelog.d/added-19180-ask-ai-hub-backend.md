- Hub operators can switch on an optional Ask AI help backend: a signed-in person who confirms a disclosure can ask a question and get a cited answer from the vendor's Chat API, called from the hub with a key held in a Secret. Off by default; with it off the routes answer 404 and nothing leaves the hub. Questions that look like credentials are refused, answers need an allowlisted docs source, and per-person, hub-wide and daily limits with a circuit breaker apply.
