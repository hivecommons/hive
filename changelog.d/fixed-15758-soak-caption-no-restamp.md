- The stage tracker's "Hive:" note for a soaking PR no longer re-stamps itself every eval pass. The contributor lane records the soak head, start and window once on the stage record and reports the stable reason `contributor-lane:soaking`; the dashboard computes the time left from that deadline at request time (showing `soak complete — merges on the next pass` at the deadline instead of `0s left`), sends an explicit `note_changed_at`, and the SPA refreshes the countdown in place rather than opening a new line. `LastSeen` no longer counts as a record change and is persisted at most once per 10 minutes. Records written with the old `contributor-lane:soaking(remaining=…)` reason stay readable (#15758).
