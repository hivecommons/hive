- Per-repo opt-in `project.repo_policies[].max_open_held_prs` caps how many open PRs the hive App authored may still be waiting on a human in one repo (#13645, part of #13617). Only hive-opened PRs that still carry a hold count; human PRs never do, and a test-only or docs-only PR stops counting once it is auto-released. At the cap `hive-open-pr` refuses new PRs in that repo and PR-opening agents are told "repo at capacity: review or close existing PRs first" in their kick; issue filing, trust levels, the trusted-merger definition and the self-merge floor are unchanged. `0` means the default of 12; unset means no cap.
