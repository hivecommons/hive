- The spoke dashboard now shows, in a findable panel at the top, which release channel the hive follows and what happened on its last upgrade attempt ([#7092](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/7092)). "Never attempted", "succeeded" and "failed" are three visually distinct states with an explicit outcome and timestamp, so a blank panel can no longer masquerade as a successful update — absence of an error is no longer treated as success. The channel is resolved from the hive's own image tag and rendered honestly: when it does not resolve to a real channel the panel says "unknown" and shows the image tag it actually observed, rather than inventing a default. A previously landed upgrade is now recorded durably (the in-flight marker was cleared the moment the new image booted, losing every success), and the view warns when the hub has not been reached recently instead of presenting stale data as current. This addresses the visibility half of #7092; choosing/changing the channel and stamping the real minor/patch version remain separate follow-ups.
