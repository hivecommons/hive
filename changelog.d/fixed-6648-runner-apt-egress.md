- Repaired apt egress on the self-hosted CI runners, which had been failing every job that installed `gcc`, `libc6-dev`, or `tmux`. The cluster has no IPv6 egress, but DNS handed apt AAAA-only records for `archive.ubuntu.com`, so it exhausted its timeout budget on unroutable addresses. apt now uses the reachable `azure.archive.ubuntu.com` mirror and forces IPv4. The manifests are recorded under `src/deploy/ci-runners/`.
