- "Let hive decide" no longer sends a PR back to an author whose clanker is offline just because someone else touched the PR. The rework-pickup signal it reads counted any change to the PR's `updated_at` as the author having "picked the rework up": a maintainer's review, a comment or a label all count. On projectbluefin/bluefin-bling#128 a maintainer's review made the hive return the PR to an author who had been offline for a day, and nothing followed for five days. Only a fix task the author's relay has taken, or a new head, counts now. An offline author falls through to the next rung, which adopts the PR. Separately, a hive decision whose effect timed out ("no effect after 30m") now shows in **Waiting on you** by the PR's real stage. Nothing re-runs such a decision, and until now the row dropped out of that list for good.
