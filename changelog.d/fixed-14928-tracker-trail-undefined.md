- The Activity Monitor hover's "Trail:" clause names each hop by stage word (e.g. "CI / Review → Needs author → Needs human decision") instead of "undefined → undefined → …": the renderer read a `stage` field the `/api/stage-tracker` history entries never carried (they emit `number`/`slug`). Hops that cannot be named are left out and the clause is omitted when fewer than two remain ([#14928](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/14928)).
