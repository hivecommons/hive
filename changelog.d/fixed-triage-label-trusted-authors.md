- On a repository where Prow or another triage bot puts `needs-triage` on every new issue, issues filed by the hive itself or by a trusted maintainer no longer sit at `stage/015-triage-needed`, in the contributor skip list and in the activity monitor's "Accept triage" asks until someone removes the label by hand. The owner decision behind 015 ([#13298](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/pull/13298)) is that triage is not a step for hive agents or trusted humans, but the stage engine and the `hub.contribute_skip_labels` default read the label without asking who filed the issue. Now the label counts only for an untrusted author: a trusted author's issue derives `stage/010-actionable` and is offered by the contributor relay, internal kicks and the good-first-issue sweep, while any other skip label (`blocked`, `epic`, `question`, the needs-decision label) still withholds it. "Trusted" means one of this hive's own accounts (its App, `ai_author`, additional hive bot logins, a hive identity), or an author the reporter-trust gate's own predicate trusts: a login in `project.issue_filter.reporter_trust.trusted_logins`, or for a human an `author_association` in `trusted_associations` (default `OWNER`, `MEMBER`, `COLLABORATOR`). It applies whether or not the gate is enabled. Another App or bot is trusted only by `trusted_logins`, never by its association, so a third-party App's issue stays held. An untrusted reporter's issue, and one whose author the hive could not see, are held at 015 and withheld as `label_skipped` exactly as before. The hive does not remove the label from trusted authors' issues. There is no new setting and no opt-out: the rule follows the existing reporter-trust trust settings.
