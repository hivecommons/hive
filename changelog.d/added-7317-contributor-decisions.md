- Hub-side decisions about a contributor (task handed back without completion, nothing available to offer, stale-generation report rejected, `task_complete` for an unheld task) are now kept in a bounded per-contributor buffer and served at `GET /api/contribute/decisions?username=<u>`, so an operator can see why a struggling contributor's reports were refused without shell access to the hub host. Previously these were slog lines on the hub process' stdout and nowhere else. ([#7317](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/7317))
