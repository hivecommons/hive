- The admin MCP can now change the advisor lane's settings, which only the dashboard's Advisor Lane panel could change until now. `advisor.settings` changes the fleet settings (on/off, model, instructions, daily budget, timeout, blocker limit), `advisor.agent` sets or clears one agent's override or opt-out, and `advisor.roles` adds, changes or removes named model roles. They go through `write_preview` and `write_confirm`, use the endpoints the panel saves with, and are owner only. The preview shows each change before and after and names turning the advisor on, raising its budget or raising its blocker limit as widening; `write_confirm` refuses if the settings changed after the preview.
