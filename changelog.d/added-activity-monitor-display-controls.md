- **Activity Monitor display controls.** In display mode (`?display=1`), moving the pointer or tabbing reveals an overlay with separate **Exit display**, **Copy link**, **Full screen** and **Keep awake** controls that hides after 3 s idle. Full screen and the screen wake lock are requested only when you click their control, never on load; unsupported and denied states render disabled or with the browser's reason and are never retried automatically. The copied link carries only `display=1`, the `am.*` view and `theme` — never a token. Nothing is persisted, and kiosk mode is unchanged ([#15929](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15929)).
