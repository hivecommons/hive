- A PR a person pushed themselves — an owner or maintainer in `dashboard.authorized_users`, or any `author/human` PR — is never adopted by the hive ([#15202](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/15202)). The contributor-lane "contributor offline" rule now applies only to clanker and registered-contributor PRs; a `requires_human` or changes-requested verdict on a human-authored PR routes to 071 Needs author and tells the author what the reviewer asked (or that the branch conflicts and needs their rebase) instead of parking at 085; and "Let hive decide" on such a PR can only approve-admit a reviewed, green head, return it to its author with the reviewer's question or the fix, or hold. The adoption path itself — the engine's `adoption/supersede` label handler, the delegate executor and the 090 "ready for human merge" decider from #15181 — refuses a person's PR with a logged reason that the stage tracker row shows as "not adopted: the hive asks them, not adopts". Folded from #15191: a DCO-less clanker head at 090 adopts in supersede mode rather than waiting on a person.
