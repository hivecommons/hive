What you’re agreeing to

No profile details are sent to any hive in this release. You can record your choices now for future syncs.

For each hive you choose, only the fields you select may be shared: your real name, organization, or country. Only non-empty values you entered about yourself are eligible. That hive’s owner and anyone with Customers access there can view and export the shared values. When a future sync matches you to a confirmed customer in that hive, the Hub learns about that customer relationship.

This feature never sends notes, Slack IDs, email, affiliation, provider-supplied profile details, or anything an administrator entered or the Hub inferred. Information already visible to hive owners through existing access lists is unaffected by these choices.

Withdrawal stops sharing immediately on the Hub. Once syncing is available, the hive removes the imported values at its next sync. Withdrawal cannot undo what someone already saw, copied, or exported.

Boxes always start unchecked; any previous selection is shown separately. Confirming replaces the entire selection for that hive. Leave nothing selected and use Withdraw consent to remove the entire selection.
