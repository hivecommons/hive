What you’re agreeing to

What is sent. For each hive you choose, only the fields you select: your real name, organization, or country. Only non-empty values you entered about yourself on this page or on the hive request form are sent, and only about you. A value an administrator entered, or a country guessed from your browser, is never sent, even if you select that field.

To whom. Only to that hive, and only when it has already confirmed you as one of its customers by your GitHub login. That hive’s owner and anyone with Customers access there can view and export the values. The hive asks by sending salted hashes of its customers’ GitHub logins, so when it matches you, the Hub learns that this hive has you as a customer.

What is never sent. Notes, email addresses, Slack IDs, affiliation, profile details from GitHub or your sign-in provider, and anything an administrator entered or the Hub inferred. Information already visible to hive owners through existing access lists is unaffected by these choices.

Withdrawing. Withdrawal, or turning a field off, stops sharing on the Hub immediately. The hive removes the values it received at its next sync, normally within an hour, and in any case within three days if it cannot reach the Hub. A value someone on the hive changed by hand is theirs and is not removed.

What withdrawal cannot undo. The Hub cannot recall a copy the hive already exported, and cannot undo what someone already saw or copied.

Boxes always start unchecked; any previous selection is shown separately. Confirming replaces the entire selection for that hive. Leave nothing selected and use Withdraw consent to remove the entire selection. When this text changes materially, sharing pauses until you confirm again.
