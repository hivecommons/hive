- Spek recheck on v6 regains the v5 proxy-only outward discovery step ([#10801](https://github.com/hivecommons/hive/issues/10801)). Exact-document sources under `runs.spektacular.recheck.sources` are read once each through the declared `discovery_proxy` (no environment or `NO_PROXY` fallback, 32 KiB UTF-8 cap, redirects refused) and land on the rewound generation as `drift.external[]` (with a `sha256` fingerprint), `external_count` and `sources_failed[]`, alongside typed `discovery` findings, whose feed reads now also go through the proxy when one is configured. An undeclared or overlay-injected destination, or a missing proxy, is rejected at config load and again at use.
