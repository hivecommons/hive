- Contributor-lane PRs no longer park at `stage/085-needs-decision` on a reviewer decision marker or bare `requires_human` while they are in CI/Review. The only early exit is a concrete security / trust finding (`review.IsSecuritySensitiveFinding`: reviewer high-risk marker, security perspective hold, security-class question, finding on a sensitive path — `sentinel.sensitive_paths` plus the new `security.sensitive_paths` — or insecure behaviour flagged), recorded as `decision_finding` and printed by the 085 comment and tracker as `the review found: …`. Everything else goes back to the clanker or is advisory and the PR moves on to the merge gate, where an untrusted author's PR parks at `stage/090-ready-for-human-merge` for the operator's merge — never auto-merged (#15640).
