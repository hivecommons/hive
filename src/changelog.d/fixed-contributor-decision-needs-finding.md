- Contributor-lane PRs no longer park at `stage/085-needs-decision` on a reviewer decision marker alone while they are in CI/Review. The review aggregate honours a `decision_requested` verdict on a non-hive-authored PR only when the review found something — a duplicate, a trust-surface / risk-class question, the reviewer's high-risk marker, or a finding at the human threshold — and records it as `decision_finding`, which the 085 comment and the tracker print as `the review found: …`. An unbacked question is advisory: the PR keeps moving and a contributor the lane cannot vouch for waits at `stage/090-ready-for-human-merge` under the human-merge rule instead.
